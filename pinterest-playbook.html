Current Pinterest Brand Identity

This playbook follows the live Pinterest Hub identity, not the older navy, teal, and gold carousel system. Pinterest pins use a saturated purple base with red Pinterest energy, bright blue utility accents, gold emphasis, and pink category accents.

Visual Direction

  • Deep purple canvas with stacked darker purple panels.
  • Bold white heavy sans-serif headlines.
  • Bright blue for useful tips, borders, checkmarks, and section labels.
  • Red for Pinterest-ready badges, active states, and action energy.
  • Gold for emotional emphasis, statistics, and warm save prompts.
  • Pink for personal, blog, myth, and softer emotional accents.

Voice Direction

  • Direct, validating, and parent-friendly.
  • Educational without sounding clinical.
  • Specific parent moments over generic advice.
  • Support-first framing, not compliance-first framing.
  • Save-worthy takeaways that make sense without a caption.

Typography and Layout Rules

Typography

  • Use Poppins for pin graphics.
  • Headlines should be bold, rounded, and heavy, usually 800 or 900 weight.
  • Use uppercase for section labels and CTA chips.
  • Keep body copy short and scannable.

Layout

  • Top pill badge, large headline, one short subtitle.
  • Use rounded cards and two-column grids where helpful.
  • Center major headlines and left-align body content.
  • Leave generous margins so the pin does not feel crowded.

Footer and CTA

  • CTA should be save-focused or share-focused.
  • Use @spectrum_unlocked, not the dot handle.
  • Footer can include a small emoji or icon.
  • Avoid salesy CTAs unless the tool is a perfect fit.

What We Look Out For

Content Checks

  • Does the headline name a real autism parenting search intent?
  • Would a parent save this for a hard moment later?
  • Is there one clear promise?
  • Can the pin stand alone without the caption?
  • Is the advice specific, kind, and practical?
  • Does the tool mention feel earned, not forced?

Design Checks

  • Headline must be readable at thumbnail size.
  • White text must have strong contrast on purple.
  • Cards should be rounded and consistent.
  • Blue, red, gold, and pink should be accents, not clutter.
  • Icons should help scanning, not decorate randomly.
  • Pin must be native 1000 by 1500, not resized from Instagram.

Never Do This

Brand Mistakes

  • Do not use the old navy, teal, and gold system for Pinterest pins.
  • Do not use puzzle symbols or puzzle emoji.
  • Do not use the dot version of the handle on Pinterest graphics.
  • Do not use low contrast muted designs.

Copy Mistakes

  • Do not use em dashes.
  • Do not shame parents or children.
  • Do not use fear-based headlines.
  • Do not write long paragraphs.

Design Mistakes

  • Do not make text tiny.
  • Do not cram more than 5 points on one pin.
  • Do not rely on complex illustrations.
  • Do not let decorative icons overpower the message.

Pin Anatomy

Top Third

  • Pill badge like SAVE THIS CHECKLIST.
  • Large white headline with blue or gold emphasis.
  • One clear subtitle that explains the value.

Middle Third

  • 3 to 5 rounded cards, bullets, or comparison blocks.
  • Use checkmarks, numbers, simple icons, and short text.
  • Make the useful content obvious without reading everything.

Bottom Third

  • One save or share prompt.
  • Handle: @spectrum_unlocked.
  • Optional warm icon like heart, bookmark, or lightbulb.

Copilot Build Workflow

Brief Copilot

  • Give topic, parent problem, and target keyword.
  • Pick one variant from this playbook.
  • Provide 3 to 5 points.
  • State the CTA and whether a tool should be mentioned.

Build HTML

  • Single HTML file with inline CSS.
  • Canvas is exactly 1000px by 1500px.
  • Use Poppins with 800 to 900 weight for headline.
  • Use purple system and hub accent colors.

QA Before Export

  • No puzzle symbols.
  • No em dashes.
  • No tiny text.
  • Handle is @spectrum_unlocked.
  • Looks like Pinterest hub, not older SU carousels.

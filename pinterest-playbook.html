Spectrum Unlocked Pinterest System

Complete Pinterest Playbook, Branding, Pin Variants, and HTML Direction

Use this page as the single reference for Copilot when creating Spectrum Unlocked Pinterest graphics. It includes the brand identity, what to look out for, the strategy, reusable variations, copy formulas, HTML structure, and QA rules. Build pins natively at 1000 by 1500 pixels and keep the visual identity consistent.

Core Pinterest Rules

The rules that should apply to every pin, no matter the topic or format.

Canvas

  • Use 1000px by 1500px.
  • Design at native Pinterest size.
  • Do not resize Instagram graphics.
  • Keep a 60px to 80px safe margin.

Brand Feel

  • Calm, warm, premium, and parent-friendly.
  • High contrast and readable on mobile.
  • Rounded cards, soft shadows, and breathing room.
  • Educational, not clinical or cold.

Never Use

  • No puzzle symbols.
  • No puzzle emoji.
  • No em dashes in copy.
  • No tiny text or cluttered layouts.

Do and Do Not

Keep the voice warm and practical.

Do

  • Say behavior is communication.
  • Use parent-friendly language.
  • Make one clear point per pin.
  • Use specific CTAs like save this checklist.
  • Use tools only when the match is specific.

Do Not

  • Do not use fear-based copy.
  • Do not shame parents or children.
  • Do not use generic platitudes.
  • Do not overfill the pin with paragraphs.
  • Do not use puzzle symbols or em dashes.

The Pinterest Playbook

The goal is simple: create pins people save because they solve a real parenting moment.

1. Start With Search

  • Use a parent search phrase in the title.
  • Make the topic obvious in 2 seconds.
  • Prefer practical phrases over clever phrases.
  • Put the keyword in the headline or subheadline.

2. Create Save Value

  • Give a checklist, script, sign list, or reframe.
  • Make it useful without needing the caption.
  • Use 3 to 5 points max.
  • End with a save-focused CTA.

3. Keep Brand Trust

  • Warm parent voice, not a clinical voice.
  • Support-first language.
  • Do not shame behavior, parents, or children.
  • Use tools only when there is a specific fit.

What We Look Out For

This is the quality filter before any pin is published.

Content Checks

  • Does the headline name a real parent problem?
  • Is there one clear promise, not five competing ideas?
  • Would a parent save this for later?
  • Can the content stand alone without a caption?
  • Is it specific enough to avoid sounding generic?
  • Does it avoid fear, shame, and absolute claims?

Design Checks

  • Is the headline readable on a phone?
  • Is the top third strong enough to stop the scroll?
  • Is there enough spacing around every text block?
  • Are teal and gold used as accents, not decoration overload?
  • Does the footer branding stay consistent?
  • Are all edges inside the safe margin?

Pin Anatomy

Every strong Spectrum Unlocked pin should follow this structure.

Top Third

Purpose: Stop the scroll.

  • Small category badge.
  • Large keyword-rich headline.
  • One emotional or practical hook.

Middle Third

Purpose: Deliver the value.

  • 3 to 5 cards or callouts.
  • Short phrases, not paragraphs.
  • Icons should be simple checks, numbers, or labels.

Bottom Third

Purpose: Drive the save.

  • Specific save or share CTA.
  • Consistent Spectrum Unlocked lockup.
  • Handle visible but not overpowering.

Copilot Build Workflow

Give Copilot these steps so it produces complete usable HTML, not just a visual idea.

Brief

  • Topic and parent problem.
  • Pin variant to use.
  • 3 to 5 content points.
  • CTA and tool mention, if any.

Build

  • Create a single HTML file.
  • Use inline CSS.
  • Canvas must be 1000 by 1500.
  • Use Google Fonts for Playfair Display and Poppins.

Check

  • No puzzle symbols.
  • No em dashes.
  • No tiny text.
  • Footer brand lockup present.

Final QA Checklist

Before publishing or exporting the pin, check every item.

Must Pass

  • 1000 by 1500 native layout.
  • Headline readable at thumbnail size.
  • 3 to 5 main points only.
  • Strong contrast across all text.
  • Brand lockup and handle included.
  • CTA is specific and save-focused.

Red Flags

  • More than 65 words on one pin.
  • Headline needs context to make sense.
  • Graphic looks like an Instagram resize.
  • Too many decorative shapes.
  • Generic advice with no parent moment.
  • Tool plug feels forced.

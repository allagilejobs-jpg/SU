# Day 40: Therapy Series - Speech Therapy

## Instagram Caption

"But my child can talk, why would they need speech therapy?"

Because speech therapy isn't just about talking. 💬

For autistic kids, speech therapy addresses:
🗣️ Expressive language (putting words together)
👂 Receptive language (understanding others)
🤝 Social/pragmatic skills (conversation, cues)
📱 AAC (alternative communication)
🔤 Articulation (being understood)
🍎 Feeding and swallowing

And here's the truth about AAC: It does NOT prevent speech. Research shows it often HELPS verbal language develop.

Every child deserves to communicate. Every child deserves to be heard.

## Instagram Caption

📚 Extended School Year (ESY): What every autism parent should know

Summer break sounds great... until your child loses months of progress in 8 weeks.

WHAT IS ESY?
Extended School Year provides special education services during summer to prevent regression. It's FREE under IDEA.

Key points:
📜 Federal right under IDEA
🎯 Focuses on maintaining skills (not new learning)
📝 Decided individually at IEP meetings
⚖️ Cannot be denied based on cost

WHO QUALIFIES?
📉 Regression: Child loses skills during breaks
⏱️ Recoupment: Takes longer to regain skills than peers
🧠 Critical learning period: Interruption would be harmful
🔄 Behaviors: Emerge or worsen during breaks
📊 Severity: Nature of disability makes ongoing services essential

HOW TO ADVOCATE:
1. Start early (discuss at IEP before spring!)
2. Bring data (videos, work samples, therapist notes)
3. Request in writing (paper trails matter)
4. Know your rights (school cannot deny based on cost)

What to say: "I am formally requesting that the IEP team discuss ESY eligibility based on documented regression."

BEYOND ESY:
📅 Year-round schools
🏠 Home programs with structure
💆 Private therapy through summer
🏕️ Specialized camps
📲 Apps and online learning
👩‍👧 One-on-one tutoring

Document everything. Data is your best advocate.

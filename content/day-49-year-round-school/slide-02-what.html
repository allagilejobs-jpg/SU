What Is ESY?
Extended School Year (ESY) provides services during summer break to prevent regression.
ESY Definition
Special education and related services provided beyond the regular school year when a child would experience significant regression without them.
  • 📜 It's a FREE service under IDEA (Individuals with Disabilities Education Act)
  • 🎯 Focuses on maintaining skills, not teaching new ones
  • 📝 Must be determined on an individual basis at IEP meetings
  • ⚖️ School cannot deny based on cost or availability

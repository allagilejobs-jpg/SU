Eligibility
Who Qualifies?
📉 Regression
Child loses skills during breaks (e.g., communication, reading, self-help skills)
⏱️ Recoupment Time
Takes longer than peers to regain lost skills after breaks
🧠 Critical Learning Period
Child is at a crucial stage where interruption would be harmful
🔄 Severe Behaviors
Behaviors that emerge or worsen during breaks and affect learning
📊 Nature of Disability
The severity of disability makes ongoing services essential
⚠️ Document Everything
Keep notes on skills lost after weekends, breaks, and summer. Data is your best advocate.

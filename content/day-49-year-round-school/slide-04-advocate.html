How to Advocate for ESY
1
Start Early
Bring up ESY at the IEP meeting BEFORE spring. Don't wait until May.
2
Bring Data
Document regression after breaks. Videos, work samples, therapist notes.
3
Request in Writing
Put your ESY request in a formal letter. Paper trails matter.
4
Know Your Rights
ESY cannot be denied due to cost. The school must consider each child individually.
💬 What to Say
"I am formally requesting that the IEP team discuss ESY eligibility for my child based on documented regression after school breaks."

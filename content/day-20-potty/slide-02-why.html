Why It Takes Longer
Traditional potty training methods often don't work for autistic kids. Here's why:
🧠
Interoception Challenges
Difficulty sensing internal body signals like "I need to go"
😰
Transition Anxiety
Change from diapers to toilet can feel overwhelming and scary
🔊
Sensory Issues
The cold seat, flushing sounds, bathroom echoes can be distressing
🔄
Routine Disruption
Breaking the diaper routine requires major adjustment

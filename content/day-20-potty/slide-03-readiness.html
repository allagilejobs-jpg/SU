Readiness Signs
Is Your Child Ready?
Age doesn't determine readiness. Look for these signs instead:
✓
Shows awareness of wet/dirty diaper (even if they don't tell you)
✓
Has predictable times for bowel movements
✓
Can stay dry for 1-2 hours at a time
✓
Shows interest in the bathroom or toilet
✓
Can follow simple 1-2 step instructions
✓
Has physical ability to sit on toilet
Important: Your child doesn't need ALL of these. Some kids learn before showing typical readiness signs.

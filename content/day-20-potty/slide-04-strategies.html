What Works
Autism-Friendly Strategies
📋
Visual Schedule
Pictures of each step: pants down, sit, go, wipe, flush, wash hands
⏰
Timed Toileting
Take them to the bathroom at regular intervals (every 30-60 min)
🎧
Address Sensory Issues
Headphones for flush noise, warm seat cover, dim lights
🎁
Motivating Rewards
Their special interest! Immediate reward for sitting or success
📱
Social Stories
Books/videos about using the potty to build understanding
🧘
Zero Pressure
No punishment for accidents. Stay calm and neutral.

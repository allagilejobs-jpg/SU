## 📸 Instagram Caption

🚽 POTTY TRAINING an autistic child: A different timeline

Throw the milestone charts away. Your child will get there. ⬇️

Why it takes longer:
🧠 Interoception challenges (hard to sense "I need to go")
😰 Transition anxiety from diapers to toilet
🔊 Sensory issues (cold seat, flushing sounds, echoes)
🔄 Routine disruption is hard

Readiness signs to look for:
✓ Awareness of wet/dirty diaper
✓ Predictable bowel movement times
✓ Can stay dry for 1-2 hours
✓ Shows interest in the bathroom
✓ Can follow simple instructions

Autism-friendly strategies:
📋 Visual schedule of each step
⏰ Timed toileting (every 30-60 min)
🎧 Address sensory issues (headphones, warm seat)
🎁 Immediate rewards using their special interest
📱 Social stories about using the potty
🧘 ZERO pressure. No punishment for accidents.

Remember:
📅 Many autistic kids aren't fully trained until 5-8+
🔄 Regression happens. It's not failure.
⏸️ Take breaks if there's extreme distress

Your child WILL get there. Don't let anyone rush you.

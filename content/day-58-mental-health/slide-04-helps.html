What Actually Helps
🧠
Autism-Informed Therapy
Find therapists who understand autism. They adapt their approach instead of forcing neurotypical expectations.
🎭
Unmask Safely
Create spaces (alone or with safe people) where you can be yourself without performing.
🔋
Honor Your Battery
Track energy levels. Rest before burnout hits. Your limits are real and valid.
🌀
Embrace Stimming
Stimming regulates your nervous system. Don't suppress it. Find stims that work for you.
👥
Find Your People
Autistic community reduces isolation. Online spaces count too!
📝
Alternative Expression
If talking is hard, try art, writing, music, or movement to process feelings.
⚠️
If You're in Crisis
988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US) - call or text 988. Crisis Text Line - text HOME to 741741. You are not alone.

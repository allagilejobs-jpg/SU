## Instagram Caption

💜🧠 Mental Health and Autism: We need to talk about this

The stats are alarming:
📊 4x higher rates of depression in autistic adults
📊 70% of autistic people experience anxiety
📊 66% have thought about suicide
📊 9x higher suicide risk than general population

This isn't because autism is a mental illness. It's because living in a world not built for us takes a real toll.

Why getting help is hard:
🎭 We mask so well that professionals miss our struggles
🩺 Most providers don't understand autism
😶 Alexithymia makes describing emotions difficult
🏥 Healthcare settings are sensory nightmares
💸 Autism-informed care is expensive and rare

What actually helps:
🧠 Autism-informed therapy (find someone who GETS it)
🎭 Unmask in safe spaces
🔋 Honor your energy limits
🌀 Embrace stimming as self-regulation
👥 Find autistic community
📝 Try alternative expression (art, writing, music)

Mental health truths:
💜 My struggles are real, not excuses
🔋 Needing rest is not weakness
🌧️ Hard days don't erase progress
🧠 I deserve support that fits my brain
✨ Healing isn't linear. Neither am I.

⚠️ If you're in crisis:
988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline - call or text 988
Crisis Text Line - text HOME to 741741

Drop a 💜 if you're working on your mental health. You're not alone.

💾 Save this for a hard day.

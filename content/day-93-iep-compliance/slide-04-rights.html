Know Your Rights
✓
IDEA protects your child — Schools must provide a free appropriate public education (FAPE)
✓
You can request an IEP meeting at any time — in writing. They must respond.
✓
Compensatory services — If services were missed, your child may be owed make-up time.
✓
Due process — You have the right to formal dispute resolution if the school won't comply.
✓
Free advocacy — Your state has a Parent Training & Information Center (PTI) that can help for free.

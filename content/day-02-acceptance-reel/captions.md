## 📸 Instagram Reels Caption

🛑 Stop saying "autism awareness"

Here's why the autistic community prefers ACCEPTANCE ⬇️

"Awareness" often:
• Implies something to warn about
• Focuses on deficits
• Historically tied to harmful orgs

"Acceptance" means:
• Recognizing autism as natural
• Celebrating differences
• Demanding action, not just knowing

The progression: Awareness → Acceptance → Inclusion → Appreciation

💙 Language matters. Acceptance changes lives.

For Job Seekers
Job Search Tips
1
Research Company Culture
Look for neurodiversity initiatives, flexible work policies, and employee reviews mentioning inclusion.
2
Practice Interview Scripts
Prepare answers for common questions. Rehearse with someone you trust. It reduces anxiety.
3
Request Accommodations Early
Ask for written questions in advance or a quiet interview room. Many companies will help.
4
Lead With Your Strengths
Focus on what you bring: attention to detail, reliability, honest communication, deep expertise.
5
Consider Autism-Friendly Employers
Microsoft, SAP, Dell, and others have specific neurodiversity hiring programs.

## Instagram Caption

💼 The employment gap for autistic adults is heartbreaking:

- 85% of autistic college grads are unemployed or underemployed
- 50% leave jobs within the first year due to lack of support
- 70% report masking at work leads to burnout

But here's what employers are missing:

Autistic employees often bring:
🎯 Deep focus and concentration
🔍 Pattern recognition others miss
💡 Creative problem-solving from unique perspectives
📋 Attention to detail and precision
🤝 Direct, honest communication
❤️ Loyalty and reliability

Simple accommodations that cost little but matter a lot:
- Noise-canceling headphones allowed
- Written instructions for tasks
- Flexible hours or remote options
- Clear expectations and direct feedback
- Meeting agendas sent in advance
- Sensory breaks when needed

For autistic job seekers:
1. Research company culture and neurodiversity initiatives
2. Practice interview scripts to reduce anxiety
3. Request accommodations early (many employers will help)
4. Lead with your strengths
5. Consider autism-friendly employers like Microsoft, SAP, Dell

The problem is not capability. It's outdated hiring practices.

What workplace accommodations have helped you? 👇

💾 Save this for your next job search!

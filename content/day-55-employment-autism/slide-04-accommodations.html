Setting Up Success
Workplace Accommodations
Simple changes that make a huge difference:
🎧
Noise Management
Quiet space, headphones allowed, reduced open-office time
💡
Lighting Control
Natural light, dimmer switches, no flickering fluorescents
📝
Written Instructions
Clear documentation instead of only verbal directions
🏠
Remote Options
Work from home days to manage sensory and social load
⏰
Flexible Hours
Adjusted start times, break schedules that work
📅
Meeting Agendas
Advance notice of topics, option to contribute in writing
🎯
Clear Expectations
Explicit goals, direct feedback, no guessing games
🧘
Decompression Time
Sensory breaks, quiet lunch spots, movement breaks
Most accommodations cost little or nothing but boost productivity significantly.

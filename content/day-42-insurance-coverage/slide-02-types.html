Coverage Types
Know Your Options
🏢
Private Insurance Most states have autism insurance mandates requiring coverage for ABA, speech, and OT. Check your state's laws.
🏛️
Medicaid Covers autism services including therapies. EPSDT requires coverage of all medically necessary services for children.
🎓
School-Based Services Free through IEP. Speech, OT, and PT provided at school. Does not replace medical therapy.
🏠
Medicaid Waivers Home and community-based services. Often has waitlists but provides additional support.

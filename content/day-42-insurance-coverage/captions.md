## 📸 Instagram Caption

🏥 INSURANCE AND THERAPY COVERAGE: What you need to know

Navigating coverage for autism therapies is overwhelming. Here's your guide ⬇️

Coverage options:
🏢 Private insurance: Check your state's autism mandate
🏛️ Medicaid: EPSDT covers medically necessary services
🎓 School services: Free through IEP (but doesn't replace medical)
🏠 Medicaid waivers: Home-based services (often has waitlists)

Commonly covered therapies:
🗣️ Speech therapy
🧩 Occupational therapy  
💪 Physical therapy
🧠 ABA therapy
👥 Social skills groups
💭 Mental health counseling

Denied? Here's how to appeal:
1️⃣ Get the denial in writing
2️⃣ Review your policy language
3️⃣ Gather supporting documentation
4️⃣ File internal appeal (meet all deadlines!)
5️⃣ Request external review if needed
6️⃣ File complaints with state insurance commissioner

Tips for success:
📞 Always verify coverage BEFORE scheduling
📝 Get pre-authorization with reference numbers
📁 Document everything
🔍 Know your state's autism mandate
🤝 Use care coordinators when available

A denial is not the final answer. Many families win on appeal.

Success Tips
Maximize Coverage
📞
Call before scheduling. Verify the provider is in-network and the service is covered.
📝
Get pre-authorization. Many therapies require prior approval. Get reference numbers.
📁
Document everything. Keep copies of evaluations, prescriptions, and all correspondence.
🔍
Know your state's mandate. Autism Speaks has a state-by-state guide to insurance laws.
🤝
Use care coordinators. Many insurers have autism care managers who can help navigate.
💡
Consider all sources. Combine insurance, school services, and grants for maximum support.

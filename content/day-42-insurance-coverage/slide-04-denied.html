Denied Coverage?
How to Appeal
A denial is not the final answer. Many families win on appeal:
1
Request denial in writing. Get the specific reason and policy language cited.
2
Review your policy. Look for autism mandates and medical necessity language.
3
Get supporting documentation. Letters from doctors, therapists, and evaluators.
4
File internal appeal. Follow their process exactly. Meet all deadlines.
5
External review. If internal appeal fails, request independent review.
6
File complaints. State insurance commissioner, CMS for Medicare/Medicaid.

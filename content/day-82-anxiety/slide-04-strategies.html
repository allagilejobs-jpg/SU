Coping Strategies
✅ Predictability: Visual schedules, advance warnings, social stories for new situations
✅ Sensory tools: Weighted blankets, noise-canceling headphones, fidgets always available
✅ Safe space: A calm-down corner they can access anytime (see Day 77!)
✅ Body-based calming: Deep breathing, heavy work, jumping, squeezing
✅ Validate first: "I can see this feels scary" before problem-solving
✅ Reduce demands: When anxiety spikes, lower expectations temporarily

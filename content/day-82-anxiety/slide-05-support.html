When to Seek Help
Home strategies are powerful, but sometimes professional support makes the difference.
📈 Anxiety is escalating despite consistent strategies at home
🏫 School refusal: They can't get through the door anymore
💔 Self-harm or talk of harm: Always take this seriously, always seek help
🌙 Daily functioning is impacted: Eating, sleeping, or hygiene are affected
Look for therapists who specialize in BOTH autism and anxiety. CBT modified for autistic brains can be very effective.

Signs to Watch For
😤
Increased Meltdowns
More frequent or intense than usual, especially after school
🚫
Avoidance
Refusing activities they used to enjoy or new situations entirely
😴
Sleep Changes
Difficulty falling asleep, nightmares, or waking up anxious
🤢
Physical Symptoms
Stomachaches, headaches, or nausea without medical cause
🔁
Increased Rigidity
Needing MORE control and routine than before
😶
Shutting Down
Going quiet, withdrawing, or becoming less communicative
Anxiety in autistic kids often looks like "behavior" — look deeper.

The Connection
Autism and anxiety are deeply intertwined — but they're not the same thing.
🧠 Sensory overload creates constant stress that the brain reads as danger
🎭 Masking all day takes enormous energy and triggers anxiety responses
🔄 Routine disruptions feel genuinely threatening when the world is unpredictable
🤝 Social expectations create constant pressure to perform neurotypically
Autistic anxiety is often a natural response to an overwhelming world — not a character flaw.

## 📸 Instagram Reels Caption

💑🧠 MARRIAGE & AUTISM PARENTING

Your partnership matters. Here's how to protect it. ⬇️

THE STRAIN IS REAL:
😴 Chronic exhaustion
💰 Financial stress from therapies
🎯 Different parenting approaches
💔 Grief processed differently
⏰ No time left for each other

This doesn't mean your marriage is failing.

3 THINGS THAT HELP:

1️⃣ SCHEDULE CONNECTION TIME
Even 15 minutes after bedtime. Phones down. Just talking.
💡 Put it on the calendar like a therapy appointment

2️⃣ GET ON THE SAME TEAM
United front with your child. Disagree privately. Never undermine each other.
💡 Weekly "strategy sessions" help a lot

3️⃣ CONSIDER COUPLES THERAPY
A therapist who understands special needs families can be transformative.
💡 It's maintenance, not crisis intervention

Your relationship is worth protecting. It's also part of your child's support system. 💕

How do you stay connected? Share below! 👇

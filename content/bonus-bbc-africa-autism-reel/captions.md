## Notes

- **Voiceover is the emotional anchor.** Take time to get it right.
- **Music sits at 25% volume** under the voiceover — just atmosphere.
- **Captions are burned in** so it works for sound-off viewers (80% of Instagram).
- **27 seconds** is optimal for Reels algorithm (longer than 15s triggers completion rate weighting; shorter than 30s keeps retention high).
- **Don't post the child's image.** The power is in the words.

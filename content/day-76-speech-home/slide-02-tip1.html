Model Language
The #1 thing speech therapists wish parents did more at home:
🗣️ Narrate your day: "I'm opening the door. I'm pouring milk." Simple, clear language.
🔄 Expand their words: Child says "ball" → You say "Yes! Big blue ball!"
⏸️ Wait time: Pause 5-10 seconds after asking. Processing takes time.
👂 Get on their level: Kneel down, make eye contact optional, be present.
Modeling > Drilling. Your child learns language from connection, not correction.

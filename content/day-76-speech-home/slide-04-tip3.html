Use AAC Consistently
AAC (Augmentative & Alternative Communication) is a bridge, not a crutch.
📲 Model AAC yourself: Use the device/board when YOU talk. Show them it's real communication.
🏠 Available everywhere: AAC should be within reach at home, school, in the car, at grandma's.
🚫 Never withhold it: AAC doesn't prevent speech — research shows it SUPPORTS it.
🔁 Be patient: It takes 12+ months of consistent modeling before expecting independent use.
Communication is a right, not a privilege. Every form of communication is valid.

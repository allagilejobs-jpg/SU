Follow Their Lead
✅ Join their play: If they line up cars, line up cars WITH them and add language
✅ Comment, don't quiz: Say "You built a tower!" instead of "What color is that?"
✅ Follow their interest: Dinosaurs? Use dino play for language practice
✅ Create communication temptations: Put favorite toy in sight but out of reach
✅ Accept all communication: Pointing, gestures, sounds, AAC — it ALL counts

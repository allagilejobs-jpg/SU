## 📸 Instagram Reels Caption

🗣️💬 SPEECH THERAPY: Beyond Just Talking

It's about communication, not just words ⬇️

COMMON MISCONCEPTION:
❌ "Speech therapy is only for kids who can't talk"
✓ SLPs help with ALL communication: verbal, nonverbal, AAC, social skills, and more!

WHAT SLPs HELP WITH:
📱 AAC (communication devices)
👋 Gestures and signing
🗣️ Verbal speech
👥 Social communication
🤔 Understanding language
🍴 Feeding/swallowing

ABOUT AAC:
AAC doesn't delay speech. Research shows it actually SUPPORTS verbal development!
💡 Communication is a RIGHT, not a privilege. AAC gives every child a voice.

MAXIMIZING SPEECH THERAPY:
✓ Practice at home (carryover is key!)
✓ Ask for home activities
✓ Observe sessions when possible
✓ Share what works at home
✓ Be patient with progress
✓ Celebrate ALL communication!

Every form of communication deserves to be heard 💙

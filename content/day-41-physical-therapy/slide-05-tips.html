Parent Tips
Making PT Work
🎮
Find the right PT: Look for therapists experienced with autism who use sensory-friendly approaches
👀
Ask to observe: Many PTs let parents watch. You'll learn techniques to use at home
🏠
Practice at home: Build movement into daily routines. Animal walks, balance games, obstacle courses
⏰
Be patient: Motor skill development takes time. Celebrate small wins
💬
Communicate: Tell the PT about sensory sensitivities, preferred activities, and what motivates your child
🤝
Team approach: PT works best when coordinated with OT and other therapies

What to Expect in PT
A typical PT journey for autistic children:
1
Evaluation: The PT assesses motor skills, balance, strength, and coordination through play-based activities
2
Goal setting: Together you'll set functional goals like climbing stairs or playing on playground
3
Sessions: Usually 30-60 minutes of fun activities disguised as therapy, often with swings, balls, and obstacle courses
4
Home exercises: Simple activities to practice between sessions
5
Progress: Regular check-ins and adjusted goals as your child develops

Does Your Child Need PT?
Signs PT Might Help
✓ Frequently trips, falls, or bumps into things
✓ Difficulty with stairs, running, or jumping
✓ Avoids playground equipment or physical play
✓ Toe walking or unusual gait patterns
✓ Poor posture or tires easily when sitting
✓ Difficulty learning to ride a bike or swim
✓ Appears "clumsy" compared to peers
✓ Low muscle tone (feels "floppy")

Understanding PT
What PT Addresses
Physical therapy helps autistic children develop motor skills that impact daily life:
🎯
Gross motor skills: Walking, running, jumping, climbing stairs
⚖️
Balance and coordination: Standing on one foot, catching a ball, riding a bike
💪
Core strength: Posture, sitting upright, body stability
🧠
Body awareness: Understanding where your body is in space
🏃
Motor planning: Coordinating movements for complex tasks

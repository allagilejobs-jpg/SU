## 📸 Instagram Caption

💪 PHYSICAL THERAPY for autistic kids is more than exercise

It's building body awareness, coordination, and confidence in movement.

Here's what you need to know ⬇️

What PT addresses:
🎯 Gross motor skills: walking, running, jumping
⚖️ Balance and coordination
💪 Core strength and posture
🧠 Body awareness (proprioception)
🏃 Motor planning

Signs PT might help:
✓ Frequently trips or falls
✓ Difficulty with stairs or jumping
✓ Avoids playground equipment
✓ Toe walking
✓ Poor posture or tires easily sitting
✓ Difficulty learning to bike or swim
✓ Low muscle tone

What to expect:
1️⃣ Play-based evaluation
2️⃣ Functional goal setting
3️⃣ Fun sessions with swings, balls, obstacles
4️⃣ Home exercise program
5️⃣ Regular progress check-ins

Parent tips:
🎮 Find a PT experienced with autism
👀 Ask to observe sessions
🏠 Practice movement at home
⏰ Be patient with progress
🤝 Coordinate with OT and other therapies

Has PT helped your child? Share your experience! 👇

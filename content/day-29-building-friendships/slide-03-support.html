How To Help
Supporting Social Connection
1
Follow Their Interests
LEGO club, coding camp, animal rescue volunteer work. Interest-based groups = natural connections.
2
Structure Playdates
Plan activities in advance. "Hanging out" is vague. "Build LEGOs for 1 hour" is clear.
3
Keep Them Short
1-2 hours max. End on a high note. Better to leave wanting more.
4
Find Neurodivergent Peers
Other autistic/ADHD kids often "get" each other instantly. Autism support groups are gold.
5
Don't Force It
If they're happy with one friend or alone time, respect that. Loneliness is internal, not external.

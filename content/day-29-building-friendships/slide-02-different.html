Friendship Looks Different
Not every autistic child wants or needs the same social connections. Valid friendship styles include:
  • 🎮 Parallel play friendships - Being together without constant interaction (gaming side by side)
  • 🎯 Interest-based connections - Bonding deeply over shared passions
  • 💬 Online friendships - Genuine connections that happen to be digital
  • 👤 One deep friendship - Quality over quantity
  • 🏠 Solitary preference - Some are genuinely content alone
Key insight: The goal isn't to make them "normal." It's to help them find connection on their terms.

## Instagram Caption

💛🤝 BUILDING FRIENDSHIPS: Social Support for Autistic Kids

Friendship looks different. That's okay.

VALID FRIENDSHIP STYLES:
🎮 Parallel play friendships (together, not constantly interacting)
🎯 Interest-based connections (bonding over shared passions)
💬 Online friendships (genuine digital connections)
👤 One deep friendship (quality over quantity)
🏠 Solitary preference (some are genuinely content alone)

The goal isn't to make them "normal." It's to help them find connection on their terms.

HOW TO SUPPORT SOCIAL CONNECTION:
1. Follow their interests (LEGO club, coding camp, etc.)
2. Structure playdates with planned activities
3. Keep playdates short (1-2 hours max)
4. Find neurodivergent peers (they often "get" each other)
5. Don't force it - respect their preferences

SOCIAL SKILLS VS. MASKING:
❌ Forcing eye contact ➡️ ✅ Let them connect naturally
❌ Pushing them to "fit in" ➡️ ✅ Find accepting spaces
❌ Criticizing how they play ➡️ ✅ Celebrate unique connecting
❌ Measuring by friend count ➡️ ✅ Measuring by happiness

When your autistic child finally finds someone who GETS them... it's everything. It might take time. Keep creating opportunities.

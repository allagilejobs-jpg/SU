## Instagram Caption

✨🎉 Celebrating AUTISTIC JOY

Because autism isn't just challenges. It's also wonder, depth, and pure magic.

What is autistic joy?
🌟 Special interests that light us up from the inside
🌀 Happy stims when we can't contain the excitement
🎵 Sensory delights that make the world feel magical
🤝 Deep connections with people who truly get us
💡 Pattern recognition that reveals hidden beauty

Moments of autistic joy (tag yourself):
📚 Getting lost for hours in a special interest
🧸 Finding the PERFECT texture
🎧 A song that hits exactly right every single time
🧩 When all the pieces click into place
🤗 Meeting someone who just GETS you
🏠 Coming home and finally unmasking
✋ Hand flapping because you're just SO happy
🔁 Rewatching your comfort show for the 47th time
💫 Infodumping to someone who actually wants to listen

How to protect your joy:
🛡️ Guard your interests from shamers
🌀 Stim without shame
🚪 Create sensory-friendly joy spaces
👥 Find joy allies who celebrate you
✨ Document joy moments for hard days

Joy affirmations:
✨ My joy is valid
🌟 My interests make life rich
🎉 I deserve to feel excitement
💃 My happy stims are beautiful
🌈 Being autistic is a gift

What brings YOU autistic joy? Share below! 🌟

📤 Share this to spread the joy!

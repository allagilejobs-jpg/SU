What Is Autistic Joy?
Autistic joy is the deep, genuine happiness that comes from being authentically ourselves.
🌟 Special interests that light us up from the inside
🌀 Happy stims when we're excited and can't contain it
🎵 Sensory delights that make the world feel magical
🤝 Deep connections with people who truly get us
💡 Pattern recognition that reveals hidden beauty
Autistic joy isn't about being "high-functioning" or performing wellness. It's about the genuine moments of wonder that our unique brains create.

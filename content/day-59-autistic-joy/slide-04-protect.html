Protect Your Joy
🛡️
Guard Your Interests
Don't let anyone shame you for what you love. Your passions are gifts, not "obsessions."
🌀
Stim Without Shame
Happy stims are self-expression. Find spaces where you can move freely.
🚪
Create Joy Spaces
Design environments where your senses can thrive. Surround yourself with good textures, sounds, and light.
👥
Find Joy Allies
Surround yourself with people who celebrate your enthusiasm, not tolerate it.
✨
Document Joy Moments
Keep a joy journal. On hard days, remind yourself what lights you up.

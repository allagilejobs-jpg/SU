## 📱 FEED CAPTION (Instagram Post)

If you don't live this life, you won't understand why I almost cried on a sidewalk because a 4-year-old pointed at a dog.

Pointing is a milestone most parents never think about. Their kid points at 9 months. At 12 months. At everything. It just happens.

FJ didn't point. Not at 12 months. Not at 18. Not at 2. Not at 3. We worked on it in speech therapy for months. Hand-over-hand. Modeling. Pointing at everything ourselves until we looked ridiculous.

Today, walking down the street, a dog passed us. FJ stopped. Extended his finger. Pointed. Looked at me. Looked back at the dog.

That's joint attention. That's him saying "Dad, do you see what I see? Isn't that cool?" That's connection.

The milestones that matter in our house aren't on any chart. They're the ones we fought for. And when they come, they hit differently because we know exactly what it took to get here.

Celebrate the small wins. They're not small.

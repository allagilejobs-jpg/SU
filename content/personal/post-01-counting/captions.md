## 📱 FEED CAPTION (Instagram Post)

FJ counts to 100 every morning before he'll eat breakfast. Same cadence. Same volume. Same spot on the kitchen floor.

For a while, I tried to redirect him. "FJ, your eggs are ready." "FJ, come sit down." He'd start over from 1 every time I interrupted.

One morning I just let him finish. 97, 98, 99, 100. He looked up, walked to the table, and ate.

That was the day I stopped seeing his routines as problems to solve and started seeing them as things that help him prepare for the world. He wasn't ignoring me. He was getting ready.

If your child has a routine that drives you crazy, try sitting with it for a week instead of fighting it. You might find out it's doing something important that you can't see.

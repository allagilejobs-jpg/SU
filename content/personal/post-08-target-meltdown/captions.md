## 📱 FEED CAPTION (Instagram Post)

Aisle 7. Target. 4:47pm on a Tuesday.

FJ was on the floor. Not screaming for a toy. Not throwing a tantrum because I said no. His nervous system hit a wall. The lights, the beeping registers, the intercom, the strangers - it all stacked up and his brain said "done."

I got on the floor with him. Didn't talk. Didn't pick him up. Just sat there.

A woman walked past and shook her head. I saw it. I felt it. I used to let that destroy me.

Then another woman, a few aisles over, caught my eye and said quietly: "Hang in there, dad."

Five words. No advice. No judgment. Just acknowledgment.

I think about her a lot. I never got her name. But she taught me something: you don't need to fix it for someone. You just need to let them know you see them.

If you see a parent on the floor of Target with a screaming child, you have two choices. One costs you nothing and means everything.

## 📱 FEED CAPTION (Instagram Post)

FJ eats 5 foods. Not 5 categories. Five specific foods prepared in a specific way.

Chicken nuggets - one brand only. Plain pasta - no sauce, not even butter. Goldfish crackers - original flavor, don't even think about the pizza ones. Applesauce - one specific pouch brand. Banana - only if it has zero brown spots.

For months I tried to expand his diet. "Just try one bite." He gagged. "It tastes the same!" It didn't to him. "He'll eat when he's hungry enough." He didn't.

Then his OT explained something that changed my perspective: for many autistic kids, food isn't just taste. It's texture, temperature, color, smell, and how it feels in their mouth all at once. A "new food" isn't just unfamiliar. It's a full sensory event that his nervous system treats as a threat.

We stopped the food battles. We keep his safe foods available. We put new foods on the table without pressure. Sometimes he touches them. Twice he licked something new. That's progress measured in months, not meals.

If your child eats 3 foods or 5 foods or 10 foods, they're eating. That's enough right now.

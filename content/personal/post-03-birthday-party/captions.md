## 📱 FEED CAPTION (Instagram Post)

We got invited to a birthday party last month. I spent 30 minutes prepping FJ. Visual schedule. Social story. Noise-canceling headphones in the bag.

We walked in. Music was loud. Kids were screaming. Balloons were popping. FJ covered his ears immediately. I picked him up. "Let's try for a few minutes."

He lasted 12 minutes. Most of it on my lap with his face in my chest. When another kid popped a balloon 3 feet from us, we were done.

On the drive home I felt the guilt hit. Why did I even try? Why can't he just enjoy a party? Why am I upset about something he doesn't even care about?

Then I checked the rearview mirror. He was calm. Humming to himself. Looking out the window. He wasn't upset. I was.

The party wasn't a failure. My expectation was the failure. He told me exactly what he needed - less noise, less chaos, more space. I just wasn't listening at first.

We'll try again. Shorter. Quieter. On his terms.

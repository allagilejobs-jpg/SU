Other autism parents, close family who truly understand. These are your 2AM people.

Respite care, trusted sitters, neighbors who can help in a pinch. Action over words.
🟡 Professional Village
Therapists, doctors, teachers, advocates. Your child's care team is YOUR team too.
💜 Online Community
Facebook groups, Instagram, forums. 24/7 understanding when you need it most.

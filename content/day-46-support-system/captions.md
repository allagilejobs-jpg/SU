## Instagram Caption

🤝 Building your support system as an autism parent

"It takes a village." Cool. But what if you don't have one?

Here's the truth:
😔 Friends fade when your life looks "too different"
🤷 Family doesn't always get it
⏰ You have no time to maintain friendships
🎭 Other parents sometimes judge

The solution? Build a NEW village. Here's how:

4 LAYERS OF SUPPORT:

💚 Inner Circle: Who GETS It
Your 2AM people. Other autism parents who understand.

🔵 Practical Support
Respite care, trusted sitters, neighbors who help in a pinch.

🟡 Professional Village
Therapists, doctors, teachers. Your child's team is YOUR team.

💜 Online Community
24/7 understanding when you need it most.

WHERE TO FIND YOUR PEOPLE:
💻 Online: Facebook groups, Instagram, Reddit, Discord
🏥 Through services: Therapy waiting rooms, school groups
🏘️ Local: Special needs leagues, sensory events
💡 Start your own: Be the friend you need

SCRIPTS THAT WORK:
"I noticed you're in the autism world too. Coffee sometime?"
"I need 2 hours Saturday. Can you help? Here's what to know..."
"I don't need advice right now. Just someone to listen."

You don't have to do this alone. Even ONE person who gets it changes everything.

Where did you find YOUR autism parent community? 👇

💾 Save this for when you're building your village

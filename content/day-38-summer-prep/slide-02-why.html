Why Summer Is Hard
🔄

Routine Changes

School schedules disappear overnight. The predictability they rely on is gone.

🌡️

Sensory Overload

Heat, sunscreen, crowds, pools, fireworks. Summer is a sensory minefield.

⏰

Unstructured Time

Open days can feel overwhelming without clear expectations or activities.

👋

Social Pressure

Playdates, camps, family gatherings. More social demands, less downtime.

Low-Stress Summer Activities
🌊
Water Play at Home
Splash pads, sprinklers, water tables. No crowded pools needed.
🌙
Early Bird Outings
Hit parks and beaches early before heat and crowds arrive.
🎨
Indoor Creative Time
Art projects, building sets, sensory bins during hot afternoons.
🌿
Nature Walks
Shaded trails, bug hunting, rock collecting. Calming sensory input.
📚
Library Programs
Free, air-conditioned, often sensory-friendly story times.
🎬
Sensory Movies
Many theaters offer sensory-friendly screenings in summer.

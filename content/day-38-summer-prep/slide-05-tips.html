Survival Tips
1
Start the summer schedule 1-2 weeks before school ends to ease the transition
2
Build in "boring days" on purpose. Not every day needs to be an adventure.
3
Keep bedtimes consistent (or close). Summer sleep chaos makes everything harder.
4
Have an exit strategy for every outing. Know when and how to leave early if needed.
5
Lower your expectations. Summer with autism is survival mode, and that's okay.

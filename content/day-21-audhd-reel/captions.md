## 📸 Instagram Reels Caption

🧠⚡ The AuDHD Experience

When your brain has two operating systems running at once ⬇️

THE CONSTANT BATTLE:

🔵 Autism says: "I need routine and sameness"
🟡 ADHD says: "I'm bored, let's do something new!"

🔵 Autism says: "Hyperfocus on this special interest forever"
🟡 ADHD says: "Abandon ship! New thing NOW"

📊 50-70% of autistic people also have ADHD

Common signs:
• Need routine but can't stick to it
• Exhausted but can't sleep
• Want to socialize but get drained
• Love planning, hate executing

If this is your child (or you), you're not broken. Your brain just works differently. 💙

What Sessions Look Like

It looks like play because it IS play:

🎢 Swinging, climbing, obstacle courses (vestibular + motor planning)
🫧 Sensory bins, playdough, shaving cream (tactile processing)
🧩 Puzzles, beading, building (fine motor)
🎮 Games that practice turn-taking, waiting, following directions
🧘 Breathing exercises, weighted activities (regulation)
"It just looks like playing!" Yes. That's the point. Kids learn best through play.

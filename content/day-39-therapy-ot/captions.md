# Day 39: Therapy Series - Occupational Therapy

## Instagram Caption

"It just looks like playing!" 

Yes. That's exactly the point. 🎯

Occupational therapy for autistic kids helps with:
✋ Fine motor skills (writing, buttons, utensils)
🌡️ Sensory processing
🚿 Self-care skills
😤 Emotional regulation
🏃 Coordination and balance
🍽️ Feeding challenges

The "occupation" for kids? Being a kid. Playing, learning, exploring. OT helps them do all of it.

If your child struggles with any of these areas, ask your pediatrician for a referral. OT can be life-changing.

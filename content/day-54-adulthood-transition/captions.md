## Instagram Caption

🎓 The transition cliff is real. Here's what you need to know.

At 18-22, autistic young adults face a sudden loss of support:

🏫 School services end at graduation
👨‍⚕️ Pediatric providers age out
📋 Adult services have years-long waitlists
🏥 Insurance coverage changes
😰 "Adult" expectations start overnight

Life skills to build NOW:
💳 Money management and budgeting
🍳 Cooking basics and grocery shopping
🏠 Home tasks and cleaning routines
🚗 Transportation options
📞 Phone calls (scripts help!)
💊 Healthcare management
🗣️ Self-advocacy
⚠️ Safety skills

Start transition planning early:
📅 Begin at 14-16, not 18
📝 Apply for adult services early (waitlists!)
📋 Gather all documentation
⚖️ Research legal options (guardianship alternatives exist)
👥 Build a support network

Redefine success:
🏠 Living situations vary, and that's okay
⏰ There's no deadline for "adulting"
💼 Work looks different for everyone
🤝 Interdependence is normal
✨ Quality of life matters most

Adulthood is a journey, not a destination. Start planning early. Redefine success on YOUR terms.

What's one thing you wish you'd known? Share below 👇

Reframe
Success Looks Different
Rethinking what "success" means for autistic adults:
🏠
Living Situations Vary
Living with family, supported housing, or solo are all valid paths.
⏰
Timelines Are Flexible
There's no deadline for "adulting." Development continues throughout life.
💼
Work Looks Different
Part-time, remote, self-employment, or day programs all count.
🤝
Interdependence Is Normal
Everyone needs support. Asking for help is a skill, not a failure.
✨
Quality of Life Matters Most
Happiness, connection, and purpose over external achievements.

What to do BEFORE the cliff:
📅
Start at 14-16
Transition planning should begin years before graduation, not months.
📝
Apply for Adult Services Early
Get on waitlists for vocational rehab and adult disability services NOW.
📋
Gather Documentation
Keep copies of all evaluations, IEPs, and medical records.
⚖️
Consider Legal Options
Guardianship alternatives, supported decision-making, power of attorney.
👥
Build a Support Network
Mentors, community connections, adult autistic role models.

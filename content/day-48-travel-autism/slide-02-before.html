Before You Go
  • 📖
    Create Social Stories
    About the trip, transportation, hotel, activities. Read daily before the trip.
  • 📸
    Visual Preview
    Show photos/videos of destination, hotel room, restaurants, attractions.
  • 📅
    Build the Schedule
    Create a visual itinerary. Include downtime. Plan for the unexpected.
  • 📞
    Call Ahead
    Hotels, airlines, attractions. Ask about accommodations and quiet spaces.
  • 🎒
    Pack the Essentials
    Comfort items, safe foods, noise-canceling headphones, favorite toys.

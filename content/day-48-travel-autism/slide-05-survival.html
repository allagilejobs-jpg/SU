While You're There
Survival Strategies
⏰
Build In Downtime
One activity, one break. Don't over-schedule each day.
🏠
Create "Home Base"
Set up hotel room with familiar items. Make it a safe space.
🍽️
Food Strategy
Bring safe foods. Research restaurants in advance. Pack snacks.
🚪
Exit Plan Ready
Know where the exits and quiet spots are at every location.
📱
Backup Entertainment
Downloaded shows, games, audiobooks for wait times.
💪
Lower Expectations
Success = everyone survived. That's it. That's the goal.
Remember: Some moments will be hard. Some will be magical. Both are okay.

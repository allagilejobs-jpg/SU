## Instagram Caption

✈️ Traveling with an autistic child: Yes, you CAN do this!

I know what you're thinking. Travel? With MY kid? But hear me out...

BEFORE YOU GO:
📖 Create social stories about the trip
📸 Show photos/videos of destination
📅 Build a visual itinerary (include downtime!)
📞 Call ahead to hotels/airlines about accommodations
🎒 Pack essentials: safe foods, headphones, comfort items

FLYING TIPS:
🎫 TSA Cares: Call 72+ hours ahead for help through security
⏰ Arrive early to avoid rushing
🎧 Pack a sensory kit (headphones, chewy, fidgets, snacks)
💺 Window for visual stimmers, aisle for movement needs
🛫 Ask about pre-boarding
📋 Carry an info card about your child's needs

AUTISM-FRIENDLY DESTINATIONS:
🏰 Theme parks (DAS passes, quiet rooms, sensory guides)
🏖️ Beach/nature (open space, sensory play, flexibility)
⚓ Cruises (contained, routine-friendly, staff support)
🏕️ Cabins/rentals (home-like, kitchen, privacy)

SURVIVAL STRATEGIES:
⏰ One activity, one break. Don't over-schedule.
🏠 Make your hotel room a "home base"
🍽️ Bring safe foods, research restaurants ahead
🚪 Know exits and quiet spots at every location
💪 Lower expectations. Survival = success!

Some moments will be hard. Some will be magical. Both are okay.

Where have you traveled with your autistic child? Share your tips! 👇

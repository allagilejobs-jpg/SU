## 📸 Instagram Reels Caption

☀️😅 SUMMER PREP for Autism Families

Summer break is coming. Let's get you ready. ⬇️

1️⃣ KEEP SOME STRUCTURE
No school doesn't mean no routine. A loose visual schedule prevents chaos.
💡 Morning routine + 1-2 planned activities + downtime

2️⃣ PLAN FOR SENSORY NEEDS
Summer = more heat, crowds, and unpredictability. Pack your sensory toolkit!
💡 Sunscreen alternatives, ear protection, portable shade

3️⃣ DON'T PAUSE THERAPY
ESY (Extended School Year) exists for a reason. Ask about summer services!
💡 Regression is real. Consistency helps.

4️⃣ SCHEDULE YOUR BREAKS TOO
You can't pour from an empty cup. Arrange respite, trades with other parents.
💡 Even 1 hour alone makes a difference

Summer doesn't have to mean survival mode. You've got this!

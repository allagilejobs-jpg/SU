## Instagram Caption

🧠⚡ AuDHD: When your brain runs on TWO operating systems

Autism + ADHD together. It's more common than you think.

Here's what you need to know:

📊 50-70% of autistic people also have ADHD traits
📊 Until 2013, doctors said you couldn't have both. They were wrong.

The internal tug-of-war:
🧩 Autism says: "I need routine"
⚡ ADHD says: "I'm bored, need novelty NOW"

🧩 Autism says: "Plan everything"
⚡ ADHD says: "Plans? What plans?"

Common AuDHD experiences:
🔄 Hyperfocus loops that shift unpredictably
😤 Emotional whiplash - big feelings, fast
🛋️ Paralysis paradox - want to do ALL things, frozen
🎭 Double masking - hiding both conditions at once
⏰ Time blindness PLUS schedule panic
🔋 Energy bursts followed by complete shutdowns

But also SUPERPOWERS:
✨ Creative problem solving
✨ Passionate expertise when both brains agree
✨ Adaptable systems that flex
✨ Deep empathy and connection

Your brain isn't broken. It's running two operating systems.

# Curated Content — Candidate Catalog

A living catalog of **everything** we can curate, brand, and repost. "Curated"
is the content pillar where we **take existing material** (interviews,
speeches, viral moments, clinical explainers, documentary clips, news
stories, long-form podcasts, court cases, legislative testimony) and
reformat it through our pipeline into SU-branded reels and carousels.

This is intentionally broad. Only a fraction will become posts. The goal is
a deep bench so we always have options.

**Legend:** 🔴 do first · 🟡 strong · 🟢 solid · 🔵 research needed · ⚫ do not curate

**Editorial guidelines:**
- Always credit the source on the cover + brand overlay
- Short excerpts only (usually under 90s)
- Never use deceased minors' stories (exploitation risk)
- Avoid anti-vax-adjacent voices permanently
- Black and diverse voices take priority (SU audience)
- Mix categories in the post queue — don't run 3 celebrity interviews back to back

2. **Download** via yt-dlp or screen-record into `content/curated/<slug>/_source-landscape.mp4`
3. **Follow** the PLAYBOOK.md new-artist checklist (section 9)
4. **Move** the entry from this file into the "Already shipped" table at the top
5. **Commit and push** the work
6. **Post** the reel and carousel to Instagram, copy captions from the artist page

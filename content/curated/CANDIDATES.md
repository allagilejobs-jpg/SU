# Curated Content — Candidate Catalog

A working catalog of everything we could curate. The roster covers:

1. **Celebrity parents** — famous people whose own children are autistic
2. **Celebrity self-advocates** — famous autistic adults talking about their own diagnosis
3. **Viral news moments** — interviews, sports moments, TV clips that went viral on their own
4. **Inspirational speakers** — graduation speeches, TED talks, public addresses
5. **Autism parent voices** — non-celebrity but highly-viewed creators and moments

Not all of these will become posts. The goal is to have a deep bench so we can always pick the strongest next one without scrambling for material.

**Legend:** 🔴 do first · 🟡 strong · 🟢 solid · 🔵 research needed · ⚫ do not curate

**Pacing:** no more than 2 curated posts per week. Mix categories to keep the feed varied. Alternate celebrity parents with viral moments and speeches so the "famous person on TV" pattern doesn't get stale.

## 3. Celebrity self-advocates — famous autistic adults

A different content angle: not a parent, but a famous person who *is* autistic and has talked about what that means for their life.

### 🟡 Anthony Hopkins — diagnosed Asperger's in 2014
- **Why:** Oscar winner (*Silence of the Lambs*, *The Father*), diagnosed in his 70s. Wife Stella first suggested it.
- **Best angle:** Late diagnosis, reframing lifelong quirks as autism traits
- **Sources:**
  - [Desert Sun 2017 — original disclosure interview](https://www.desertsun.com) (search archive)
  - [Fox News — Hopkins calls mental health labels "nonsense"](https://www.foxnews.com/entertainment/anthony-hopkins-calls-mental-health-labels-nonsense-dismisses-autism-diagnosis-from-his-wife) — complicated, handle carefully
- **Caveat:** Hopkins has been dismissive of labels (calls diagnoses "rubbish"). Frame as "late diagnosis recognition", not "autism advocacy"
- **Priority:** 🟡 Strong name recognition but complicated messaging

### 🟡 Dan Aykroyd — Asperger's, credits it with Ghostbusters
- **Why:** Legend actor, openly credits his autism with being the reason Ghostbusters exists (his obsession with ghosts and law enforcement)
- **Best angle:** "My symptoms included my obsession with ghosts and law enforcement — I carry a police badge with me" — hilariously relatable ASD trait framing
- **Sources:**
  - [NPR Terry Gross 2004 interview](https://www.npr.org) — Aykroyd discusses Asperger's, carrying the police badge
  - [Child Mind Institute — How being on the spectrum helped make Ghostbusters](https://childmind.org/blog/dan-aykroyd-says-being-on-the-spectrum-helped-him-make-ghostbusters/)
  - [The Walrus — Before Ghostbusters: How Asperger's Shaped Young Dan Aykroyd](https://thewalrus.ca/before-ghostbusters-how-aspergers-shaped-young-dan-aykroyd/)
- **Priority:** 🟡 Fun, positive angle — "autism as superpower" without being saccharine

### 🟡 Temple Grandin — the OG autism self-advocate
- **Why:** Animal science professor, author, subject of the HBO biopic, absolutely iconic 2010 TED talk "The world needs all kinds of minds" (10+ million views)
- **Best angle:** The TED talk itself is the content. Her line "The world needs all kinds of minds… if autism had been eradicated, men would still be socializing in front of a wood fire at the entrance to a cave"
- **Sources:**
  - [TED.com — The world needs all kinds of minds (2010)](https://www.ted.com/talks/temple_grandin_the_world_needs_all_kinds_of_minds)
  - [TEDxCSU — Educating different kinds of minds](https://www.ted.com/talks/temple_grandin_educating_different_kinds_of_minds)
  - [NPR — Autism advocate Temple Grandin](https://www.npr.org/transcripts/1163975062)
- **Priority:** 🟡 Iconic — the TED talk excerpt is a no-brainer

### 🔵 Elon Musk — SNL Asperger's announcement
- **Why:** Hosted SNL on May 8, 2021, joked "I'm the first person with Asperger's to host SNL" during monologue
- **Caveat:** Musk has become politically polarizing since then. Also Dan Aykroyd actually hosted earlier with Asperger's disclosed. **Musk is a landmine**. Curate Dan Aykroyd instead for the "famous autistic SNL host" angle.
- **Sources:** [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/elon-musk-aspergers-saturday-night-live/), [Slate critique](https://slate.com/culture/2021/05/elon-musk-aspergers-snl-autism-spectrum.html)

## 4. Viral news moments — the pure gold category

These are moments that went viral on their own merit. They already have proven attention, so a reel that excerpts them lands on a wave of existing cultural awareness.

### 🔴 Dan Orlovsky's son Madden on NFL Live (2025 World Autism Awareness Day)
- **What:** ESPN NFL analyst Dan Orlovsky brought his 14-year-old autistic son Madden onto NFL Live for World Autism Awareness Day. Madden's artwork decorated the studio, he chanted the Eagles fight song, and gave an unscripted message: *"I want to tell this to Hunter, Noah, Lennon and Mom, if they're watching this. Mom, I love you. Hunter, you're my favorite twin. Noah, I do like you. And Lennon, you're a good sister."* Dan burst into tears on-air. Magic Johnson, Ric Flair, Pat McAfee all reacted publicly.
- **Why:** This is THE curated content goldmine of 2025. Genuine, unscripted, emotional, from a dad audience (NFL), with a child speaking directly into camera about the people he loves.
- **Sources:**
  - [Today — Dan Orlovsky brings son Madden to NFL Live](https://www.today.com/parents/dads/dan-orlovsky-son-madden-viral-espn-world-autism-awareness-day-rcna266584)
  - [Yahoo Sports — Orlovsky emotional reaction](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/dan-orlovsky-emotional-sons-world-234616600.html)
  - [Front Office Sports](https://frontofficesports.com/dan-orlovsky-son-nfl-live-appearance-world-autism-awareness-day/)
  - ESPN Instagram — video of the segment (primary source to download)
- **Priority:** 🔴 DO THIS NEXT. Build it before the moment cools.

### 🟡 Jason McElwain "J-Mac" — 20 points in 4 minutes (Feb 2006)
- **What:** Autistic high school basketball manager got put in the last 4 minutes of senior night. Scored 20 points (six 3-pointers and one 2-pointer). Got an ESPY, met President Bush, became a national story.
- **Why:** Classic inclusive-sports moment, pre-social-media viral (the original "feel-good autism story" on TV news)
- **Best angle:** Contextualize for 2026 — "19 years later, J-Mac is still inspiring"
- **Sources:**
  - [ESPN — J-Mac: The game nobody could forget](https://www.espn.com/espn/story/_/id/14780896/jason-mcelwain-changed-lives-inspired-autistic-community-20-point-game-10-years-ago)
  - [CBS News — 10 years later check-in](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/on-the-road-checking-in-with-viral-basketball-star-jmac-10-years-later/)
  - [WXXI News 2025 — J-Mac 19 years later](https://www.wxxinews.org/local-news/2025-02-16/j-mac-continues-to-shine-a-light-on-perseverance-nearly-two-decades-after-his-high-school-basketball-feat)
  - YouTube: original game footage widely available
- **Priority:** 🟡 Evergreen — works any time of year

### 🟡 Elizabeth Bonker — nonspeaking valedictorian commencement speech (Rollins College, 2022)
- **What:** Nonspeaking autistic valedictorian at Rollins College delivered her commencement address via text-to-speech. Line that hit hardest: *"The irony of a nonspeaking autistic encouraging you to use your voice is not lost on me. Because if you can see the worth in me, then you can see the worth in everyone you meet."*
- **Why:** Highest-impact "autism can do anything" moment from 2022. Quote is a t-shirt line.
- **Sources:**
  - [NPR — nonspeaking valedictorian speech](https://www.npr.org/2022/05/12/1098506522/nonspeaking-valedictorian-autism-college-commencement-speech)

  - [GMA — moving commencement speech](https://www.goodmorningamerica.com/living/story/nonspeaking-student-autism-moving-commencement-speech-84644347)
  - YouTube: speech video widely available
- **Priority:** 🟡 Cannot-miss content

### 🟡 Ben Gretchko — Seaholm High School graduation speech (2017)
- **What:** Autistic high school valedictorian's speech "In fact, I am autistic. Don't give up. Don't let anybody tell you what you can and can't do." Got 1.6M+ views on Autism Speaks Facebook.

## Process for promoting a candidate

When ready to build one:

1. **Verify** the story/quote is real (search 2 independent confirmations). Never publish on tabloid rumor alone.
2. **Download** the source via yt-dlp (or screen-record if it's Instagram-only). Save to `content/curated/<slug>/_source-landscape.mp4`
3. **Follow** the PLAYBOOK.md new-artist checklist (section 9)
4. **Move** the entry from this file into the "Already shipped" table at the top
5. **Commit + push** the work
6. **Post** the reel and carousel to Instagram, copy captions from the artist page

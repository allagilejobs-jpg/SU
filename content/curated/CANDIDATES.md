## Process for adding a new candidate

When you pick one:

1. **Verify publicly-confirmed autism story** — find at least 2 independent interviews where the celebrity themselves says their child is on the spectrum. Never publish based on tabloid rumors.
2. **Locate the best source clip** — usually the one with the strongest quotable moment, 5–15 min length, on YouTube.
3. **Add them to `docs/outreach-list.html`** if Outreach/Collab is the goal as well.
4. **Run through the PLAYBOOK.md new-artist checklist** (section 9).
5. **Update `CANDIDATES.md`** to move them from this list into the "already shipped" table at top.

## Notes on pacing

Don't publish more than 2 curated posts per week. The genre gets stale fast and each post needs space to breathe algorithmically. Rotate with:

- **Original educational** carousels (IEP, therapy types, sensory hacks) — highest volume, easy to batch
- **Personal founder stories** — 1 per month, monthly anchor
- **Seasonal** — tied to Autism Month (April), Back-to-School (August), IEP season (September–October)

The curated posts are the **social proof anchors** — you want them to feel special, not grind-y. A new celebrity every 2–3 weeks is the right cadence.

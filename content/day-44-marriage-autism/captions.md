## 📸 Instagram Caption

💑 MARRIAGE AND PARTNERSHIP when parenting autism

The unique pressures on relationships. How to stay connected ⬇️

The hard truth:
📊 80% of couples report increased stress after diagnosis
📊 2x higher burnout rates than other special needs parents
💚 But many couples become STRONGER through it

Common challenges:
⏰ No time together
😴 Constant exhaustion
⚖️ Unequal mental load
🎯 Different parenting approaches
💔 Processing grief differently
🏝️ Social isolation

What actually helps:
📅 Schedule couple time (even 15 minutes counts)
🤝 Present as a united team
📊 Share the load clearly
💬 Check in: "How are YOU doing?"
🎯 Regular meetings about approach
🙌 Give each other solo breaks

When to get help:
💭 Couples therapy (find someone who gets special needs parenting)
👥 Support groups for autism parent couples
🧘 Individual therapy
🏠 Respite care for time away together

Red flags: constant criticism, contempt, stonewalling, defensiveness. Seek help early.

Your relationship matters. A strong partnership makes better parents.

What keeps your partnership strong? 👇

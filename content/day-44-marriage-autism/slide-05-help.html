Getting Support
When to Get Help
There's no shame in needing professional support:
💭
Couples therapy: Find someone who understands special needs parenting
👥
Support groups: Connect with other autism parent couples
🧘
Individual therapy: Each partner may need their own space to process
🏠
Respite care: Time away together can reset everything
Red flags: Constant criticism, contempt, stonewalling, or defensiveness. These predict relationship breakdown. Seek help early.

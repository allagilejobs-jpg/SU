Staying Connected
What Actually Helps
📅
Schedule couple time: Even 15 minutes after kids sleep. Put it in the calendar like an appointment.
🤝
Present as a team: Make major decisions together. Back each other up publicly.
📊
Share the load: Divide responsibilities clearly. Rotate the hard tasks.
💬
Check in regularly: "How are YOU doing?" Ask it every day. Mean it.
🎯
Align on approach: Regular meetings about therapies and goals. Be on the same page.
🙌
Give each other breaks: Solo time for each partner is not selfish. It's survival.

## 📸 Instagram Reels Caption

🌍🌿 NATURE & AUTISM: Free Therapy Outside

Happy Earth Day! Fresh air might be the best sensory tool you're not using ⬇️

WHY NATURE HELPS:
🔇 Less artificial noise
💡 Natural lighting (no fluorescents!)
🌬️ Fresh air regulates the nervous system
🏃 Space to move freely
🧘 Calming sensory input
🌳 No social scripts required

SENSORY-FRIENDLY ACTIVITIES:
🪨 Collect rocks, leaves, sticks
💧 Water play at a creek
🐛 Bug watching
🌸 Smell different flowers
🥾 Quiet trail walks
🌈 Cloud watching

MAKING IT WORK:
✓ Go during quiet hours (early AM)
✓ Bring sensory tools
✓ Start with short trips (15-20 min)
✓ Let THEM lead the exploration
✓ No agenda or forced activities

This Earth Day, give your child the gift of nature-based regulation 💚

Why This Matters
Withholding AAC from a child who "can talk" is like taking away a wheelchair from someone who can stand for a few minutes. Just because they CAN doesn't mean it's sustainable, reliable, or comfortable. AAC reduces frustration, supports regulation, and gives every child MORE ways to be heard.

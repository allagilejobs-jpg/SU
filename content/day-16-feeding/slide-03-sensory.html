Sensory Factors
It's Not Just Taste
👁️
Visual
Color, shape, or foods touching each other can trigger refusal
👃
Smell
Strong or unfamiliar scents can be overwhelming before food even reaches the mouth
🖐️
Texture
Slimy, crunchy, mushy, or mixed textures can feel physically painful
🌡️
Temperature
Some kids only tolerate foods at specific temperatures
👂
Sound
Crunching, slurping, or chewing sounds (their own or others')
🧠
Interoception
Difficulty recognizing hunger, fullness, or thirst signals

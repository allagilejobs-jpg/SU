## 📸 Instagram Caption

🍽️ FEEDING CHALLENGES in autistic kids go way beyond "picky eating"

It's not defiance. It's not bad parenting. It's sensory.

Here's what you need to know ⬇️

📊 70-90% of autistic children have feeding challenges
📊 They're 5x more likely to have a limited diet

It's not just about taste:
👁️ Visual - colors, shapes, foods touching
👃 Smell - can be overwhelming before it reaches the mouth
🖐️ Texture - some textures feel physically painful
🌡️ Temperature - specific requirements
👂 Sound - their own chewing can be distressing

What actually helps:
✅ Use divided plates (foods don't touch!)
✅ Same foods, same time (predictability)
✅ Remove ALL pressure (no "just one bite")
✅ Food chaining (bridge from safe foods)
✅ Food play without eating expectations

When to seek help:
⚠️ Weight loss or failure to gain
⚠️ Eating fewer than 10-15 foods
⚠️ Extreme distress around food
⚠️ Nutritional deficiencies

You're not a bad parent. Your child's brain experiences food differently.

What's your child's safe food? 👇

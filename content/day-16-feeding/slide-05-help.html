When to Get Support
Seek Professional Help If...
Some feeding challenges need expert support. Watch for these signs:
⚠️ Red Flags
  • 📉 Weight loss or failure to gain weight appropriately
  • 🍎 Eating fewer than 10-15 different foods total
  • 😰 Extreme distress, gagging, or vomiting around food
  • 💊 Nutritional deficiencies (fatigue, pallor, hair loss)
  • 🚫 Dropping foods without adding new ones
👩‍⚕️ Who Can Help
Feeding therapists, occupational therapists, pediatric dietitians, and SOS (Sequential Oral Sensory) trained specialists.

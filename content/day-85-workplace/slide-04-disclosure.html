To Disclose or Not?
Reasons to Disclose
✅ Access to legal protections
✅ Get accommodations you need
✅ Reduce the exhaustion of masking
✅ Colleagues may understand you better
Reasons to Wait
⚠️ Stigma still exists in many workplaces
⚠️ Not every manager handles it well
⚠️ You can request some changes informally
⚠️ It's deeply personal — there's no "should"
There is no wrong answer. Disclosure is YOUR choice, on YOUR timeline. You can disclose to HR without telling your whole team.

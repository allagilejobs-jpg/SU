## 📸 Instagram Reels Caption

🏕️🧠 SUMMER CAMP for Autistic Kids

Finding the right fit (and making it work) ⬇️

CAMP OPTIONS:
🎯 Specialized Camps - Designed specifically for autistic kids. Trained staff, built-in supports.
🤝 Inclusive Camps - Mainstream camps with inclusion programs. May offer 1:1 aides.
🌿 Therapeutic Camps - OT, speech, or social skills focused. Learning while having fun!

QUESTIONS TO ASK:
❓ Staff-to-camper ratio?
❓ Training on autism/sensory needs?
❓ Quiet spaces available?
❓ How are meltdowns handled?
❓ Can I provide a sensory profile?
❓ Communication with parents?
❓ Food accommodations?

PREPARING YOUR CHILD:
✓ Visit the camp beforehand
✓ Create a social story about camp
✓ Practice the drop-off routine
✓ Pack comfort items
✓ Start slow (half days first)
✓ Have a backup plan

The right camp can be magical. The wrong one can be traumatic. Do your research! 💙

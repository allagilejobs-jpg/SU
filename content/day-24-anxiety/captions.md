## Instagram Caption

😰 Autism and Anxiety: The hidden connection

Most autistic people experience anxiety. Here's what you need to know:

The stats:
📊 40-80% of autistic people have clinical anxiety
📊 50% of autistic children have an anxiety disorder
📊 Anxiety is the #1 co-occurring condition with autism

Common triggers:
🔊 Sensory overload
🔄 Unexpected changes
👥 Social uncertainty
🎭 Masking exhaustion
❓ Ambiguity and uncertainty
⏰ Transitions between activities
📱 Too many demands
💭 Interoception issues (can't identify body signals)

What actually helps:
📅 Predictability - visual schedules, advance warnings
🏠 Sensory safe spaces - quiet corner, dim lights, headphones
🌀 Allow stimming - it's regulating, not a problem
🎯 Special interests - diving in reduces cortisol
📝 Scripts and preparation - practice, preview, plan
⚡ Reduce demands when stressed - recovery matters

When to get professional help:
🔍 Find autism-informed therapists (CBT may need mods)
💊 Medication can be part of the plan
🧘 OT for sensory regulation

⚠️ Red flags: school refusal, self-harm, isolation, panic attacks, physical symptoms, skill regression

Anxiety in autism isn't overreacting. Their nervous system is genuinely perceiving more threats.

What helps YOUR child (or you) with anxiety? 👇

Professional Help
When to Seek Support
🔍 Find Autism-Informed Therapists
Not all anxiety treatment works for autism. CBT may need modifications. Look for neurodiversity-affirming providers.
💊 Medication Can Help
SSRIs or other medications can be part of the plan. Work with a psychiatrist who understands autism.
🧘 OT for Sensory Regulation
Occupational therapy helps build sensory strategies that prevent anxiety from building up.
⚠️ Seek Help If You See:
School refusal, self-harm, isolation, panic attacks, physical symptoms (stomach aches, headaches), loss of skills, or talk of hopelessness.

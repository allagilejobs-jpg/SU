## 📸 Instagram Reels Caption

👧💔👦 THE GLASS CHILD: A Follow-Up

Don't let your neurotypical child become invisible ⬇️

QUICK REMINDER:
"Glass children" are siblings who feel they must be perfect and invisible because attention goes to their sibling with higher needs.

They SEE everything, but feel like they're SEEN THROUGH.

3 WAYS TO SUPPORT THEM:

1️⃣ SCHEDULED 1-ON-1 TIME
Put it on the calendar. Make it non-negotiable. Even 15 minutes counts. Their choice of activity, always.

2️⃣ VALIDATE THEIR FEELINGS
"It's okay to feel frustrated/sad/angry. Your feelings matter too." Never shame them for struggling with their sibling.

3️⃣ CONNECT WITH PEERS
Sibling support groups exist! Kids who "get it" help SO much. Check: Sibling Support Project, local autism organizations.

Remember: They need permission to be imperfect too.

How do you support your glass child? Share your tips below! 👇

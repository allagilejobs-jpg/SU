Why It Happens
The Brutal Reality
01
No Diagnosis
In many African countries, "autism" has no word in local languages. Families don't know what they're facing.
02
No Services
Therapy, schools, and respite care are rare or nonexistent. Families have no lifeline.
03
Deep Stigma
Autism is often blamed on witchcraft or curses. Children are hidden to protect families from shame.
04
No Support
Parents carry it alone. No village. No resources. No one to call when things get hard.

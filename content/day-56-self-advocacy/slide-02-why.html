Why Self-Advocacy Matters
No one knows your needs better than you. But speaking up can feel impossible when the world wasn't designed for your brain.
🎭 Masking drains us: When we hide our needs, we exhaust ourselves trying to fit in
❓ Others can't read minds: People often want to help but don't know how unless we tell them
⚖️ Accommodations are rights: You deserve support, not as a favor, but as equal access
Remember: Self-advocacy isn't selfish. It's how you create a life that actually works for YOU.

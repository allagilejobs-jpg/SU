Build Your Skills
5 Advocacy Tips
01
Know Your Rights
Research disability laws in your area. Knowledge is power.
02
Start Small
Practice with low-stakes situations first. Build confidence gradually.
03
Write It Down
If speaking is hard, email or text your needs instead.
04
Bring a Buddy
A trusted person can advocate alongside you in tough conversations.
05
It's Okay to Walk Away
If someone refuses to respect your needs, you can disengage. Self-protection is self-advocacy too.

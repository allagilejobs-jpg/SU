Scripts You Can Use
At Work or School
I work best when I have written instructions. Can you send that in an email?
At the Doctor
I need extra time to process questions. Please give me a moment before expecting a response.
With Friends/Family
I care about you, but I need to recharge alone right now. It's not personal.
In Loud Environments
The noise level is overwhelming for me. Can we move somewhere quieter?

Pro tip: Practice these scripts before you need them. Write them down. Having words ready reduces the pressure in the moment.

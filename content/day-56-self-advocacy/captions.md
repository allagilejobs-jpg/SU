## Instagram Caption

🗣️ Self-Advocacy 101: Skills every autistic person needs

No one knows your needs better than you. But speaking up can feel impossible when the world wasn't designed for your brain.

Why it matters:
🎭 Masking drains us when we hide our needs
❓ Others can't read minds, they need us to tell them
⚖️ Accommodations are RIGHTS, not favors

Scripts you can use (save these!):

💼 At work: "I work best when I have written instructions. Can you send that in an email?"

🏥 At the doctor: "I need extra time to process questions. Please give me a moment."

👥 With family: "I care about you, but I need to recharge alone. It's not personal."

🔊 In loud spaces: "The noise level is overwhelming. Can we move somewhere quieter?"

5 advocacy tips:
1️⃣ Know your rights - research disability laws
2️⃣ Start small - build confidence gradually
3️⃣ Write it down - email if speaking is hard
4️⃣ Bring a buddy - for tough conversations
5️⃣ It's okay to walk away - self-protection counts

Affirmations:
🗣️ My needs are valid
✊ I deserve accommodations
💪 Asking for help is strength
🌟 I am the expert on myself

What's one thing you wish you could advocate for? Tell us below 👇

💾 Save these scripts for when you need them!

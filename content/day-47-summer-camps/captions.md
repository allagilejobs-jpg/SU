## Instagram Caption

🏕️ Summer camp with an autistic child: What to know before you enroll

Summer is coming. Camp brochures are everywhere. But how do you find the RIGHT fit?

TYPES OF CAMPS:
🌟 Specialized autism camps: Trained staff, structured, sensory-friendly
🤝 Inclusive camps: Mixed populations with support staff
⚽ Traditional + accommodations: Regular camps willing to adapt
🏠 Day vs overnight: Start small, build up

QUESTIONS TO ASK:
1. What autism/sensory training do staff have?
2. What is the camper-to-staff ratio?
3. Is there a quiet space for sensory breaks?
4. How do you handle meltdowns?
5. Can I provide visual schedules or social stories?
6. What is your parent communication plan?
7. Can we visit before the first day?

PREPARING YOUR CHILD:
📖 Create social stories about camp
📸 Get photos in advance (location, counselors, activities)
🎒 Pack a comfort kit (fidgets, headphones, favorite snack)
🚗 Practice runs: Drive by, do a tour, practice morning routine

Timeline: Start prep 4 weeks out!

🚩 RED FLAGS:
"We treat all kids the same" (no accommodations)
No quiet space available
Punitive approach to behaviors
Resistant to parent input

💚 GREEN FLAG:
"Tell us about YOUR child. What works for them?"

The right camp exists. Keep looking until you find it.

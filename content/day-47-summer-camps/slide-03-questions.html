Ask Before Enrolling
Questions to Ask
1
What training do staff have in autism and sensory needs?
2
What is the camper-to-staff ratio?
3
Is there a quiet space for sensory breaks?
4
How do you handle meltdowns or dysregulation?
5
Can I provide a visual schedule or social story?
6
What is your communication plan with parents?
7
Can we visit before the first day?
Pro tip: If they hesitate on these questions, it might not be the right fit.

Types of Camps
🌟
Specialized Autism Camps
Staff trained in autism, structured activities, sensory-friendly environments
Best for: High support needs, first-time campers
🤝
Inclusive Camps
Neurotypical + neurodivergent kids together with trained support staff
Best for: Social skill building, moderate support needs
⚽
Traditional + Accommodations
Regular camps willing to make accommodations for your child
Best for: Lower support needs, interest-based (art, sports, tech)
🏠
Day vs. Overnight
Start with day camps if routine changes are hard. Work up to overnight.
Know your child's limits and build gradually

Healthy Use
Finding Balance
Tips for a healthy relationship with technology:
⏰
Set Time Boundaries
Use timers and app limits. Hyperfocus can make hours disappear.
🌙
Protect Sleep
Blue light filters and screen-free wind-down routines help.
🚶
Movement Breaks
Set reminders to stand, stretch, or walk away from screens.
🎯
Intentional Use
Choose what serves you. Curate feeds, mute noise, unfollow stress.
🤝
Online + Offline
Online friends are real friends. In-person time is also important.

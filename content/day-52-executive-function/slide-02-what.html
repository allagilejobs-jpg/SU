What Is Executive Function?
Executive functions are the brain's management system:
🎯
Planning
Breaking tasks into steps and sequencing them
🚀
Initiation
Actually starting tasks (the hardest part!)
🔄
Flexibility
Switching between tasks and adapting to change
💭
Working Memory
Holding info while using it
🛑
Impulse Control
Pausing before acting or speaking
⏰
Time Awareness
Sensing how time passes and estimating duration
😤
Emotional Regulation
Managing emotional responses
📊
Organization
Keeping track of things and priorities

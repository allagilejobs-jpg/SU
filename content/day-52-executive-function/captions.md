## Instagram Caption

🧠 Executive function: the invisible struggle

You know that feeling when you WANT to do something but your brain just... won't?

That's executive function. And autistic brains often run this system differently.

What IS executive function?

🎯 Planning: breaking tasks into steps
🚀 Initiation: actually STARTING (the hardest part!)
🔄 Flexibility: switching between tasks
💭 Working memory: holding info while using it
🛑 Impulse control: pausing before acting
⏰ Time awareness: sensing time passing
😤 Emotional regulation: managing responses
📊 Organization: keeping track of things

What this looks like in real life:

🛋️ "I know I need to do it but I can't start" (task paralysis)
⏱️ "5 minutes" becomes 2 hours (time blindness)
🔀 Transitions feel impossible
🗂️ Everything feels equally urgent
🧠 Forgetting mid-task constantly

Strategies that actually help:

📝 Write EVERYTHING down
⏲️ Visual timers (make time visible)
🎯 Body doubling (someone nearby while you work)
🔔 Transition warnings ("5 minutes until...")
🧩 Break tasks into TINY steps

This isn't laziness. It's a different operating system that needs different tools.

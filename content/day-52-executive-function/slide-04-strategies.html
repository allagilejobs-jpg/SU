What Helps
Practical Strategies
External systems to support internal struggles:
📝
Write Everything Down
External memory. Lists, notes, reminders. If it's not written, it doesn't exist.
⏲️
Visual Timers
Make time visible. Seeing time helps when you can't feel it passing.
🎯
Body Doubling
Having someone nearby (even virtually) while you work on tasks.
🔔
Transition Warnings
"5 minutes until we leave" gives the brain time to shift gears.
🧩
Break Tasks Into Tiny Steps
Not "clean room" but "put 3 things away." Micro-tasks reduce paralysis.

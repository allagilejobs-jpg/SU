IEP Meeting Prep Checklist
Before the Meeting
Review current IEP goals and progress
Write your parent concerns statement
Gather work samples and data
Prepare questions for the team
During the Meeting
Request Prior Written Notice for decisions
Take notes or bring a support person
Don't sign anything you haven't read
After the Meeting
Send follow-up email confirming decisions
Set calendar reminders for deadlines

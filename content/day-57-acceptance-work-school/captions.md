## Instagram Caption

💼🎓 Autism acceptance at work and school: What it REALLY looks like

The numbers are stark:
📊 85% of college-educated autistic adults are unemployed or underemployed
📊 50% of autistic students report bullying
📊 70% of autistic employees mask heavily at work

The problem isn't us. It's systems that weren't designed with us in mind.

🚫 Performative "awareness" looks like:
- Puzzle piece logos with no action
- "We accept everyone" but no real accommodations
- One autism month event, then silence
- Expecting us to "fit in" without changes

💚 Real acceptance looks like:
- Proactive accommodations built in
- Flexible work and learning options
- Quiet spaces and sensory support
- Autistic people in leadership and planning
- Accommodations seen as NORMAL

What actually helps:

At work:
📝 Written instructions over verbal
🏠 Remote/hybrid options
🎧 Noise-canceling headphones allowed
💡 Adjustable lighting
⏰ Flexible start times

At school:
⏳ Extended time on tests
🪑 Preferential seating
📋 Clear rubrics
🚶 Movement breaks allowed

Remember: These aren't "special treatment." They're equal access.

For allies: Listen first. Amplify, don't speak over. Advocate when we can't. Keep learning from autistic voices.

What's one accommodation that changed YOUR life? Share below 👇

📤 Share this with someone who needs to see it!

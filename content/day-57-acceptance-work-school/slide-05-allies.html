For Allies
How to Be Supportive
👂
Listen First, Assume Nothing
Every autistic person is different. Ask what helps instead of guessing.
🔊
Amplify, Don't Speak Over
Support autistic voices. Don't become the spokesperson.
🛡️
Advocate When We Can't
Push for policy changes. Challenge ableism when you see it.
📚
Educate Yourself
Follow autistic creators. Read our perspectives, not just "expert" opinions about us.
The best allies: Create space, share power, and keep learning. Acceptance is an ongoing practice, not a checkbox.

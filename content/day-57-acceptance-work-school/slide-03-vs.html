Know the Difference
Awareness vs Acceptance
🚫 Performative Awareness
Puzzle piece logos with no action
"We accept everyone" but no accommodations
Hiring autistic people, then not supporting them
One autism month event, then silence
Expecting us to "fit in" without changes
Training about autism without autistic voices
Treating requests as "special treatment"
→
💚 Real Acceptance
Proactive accommodations built in
Flexible work arrangements for all
Quiet spaces and sensory options
Year-round inclusion efforts
Changing systems, not people
Autistic people in leadership and planning
Accommodations seen as normal

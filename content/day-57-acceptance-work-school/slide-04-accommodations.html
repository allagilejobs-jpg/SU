Accommodations That Work
💼 At Work
📝 Written instructions over verbal
🏠 Remote or hybrid options
🎧 Noise-canceling headphones allowed
💡 Adjustable lighting at desk
⏰ Flexible start times
🚪 Quiet space for breaks
🎓 At School
⏳ Extended time on tests
🪑 Preferential seating options
📋 Clear assignment rubrics
🚶 Movement breaks allowed
🎒 Sensory tools permitted
📧 Email instead of verbal instructions

Remember: These aren't "special treatment." They're equal access. A ramp for a wheelchair user isn't a luxury. Neither are sensory accommodations.

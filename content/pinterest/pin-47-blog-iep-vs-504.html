📋 IEP vs 504

Which one does your kid need?

✕ The Confusion
"An IEP and a 504 are basically the same thing."
They sound similar. They're not. Picking the wrong one can leave your child without the support the law guarantees.
VS
✓ The Truth
An IEP teaches differently. A 504 just accommodates.
📚
IEP = specialized instructionIndividualized goals. Speech, OT. Legal protection under IDEA.
🛠️
504 = accommodations onlyNo specialized teaching. Less paperwork. Faster to set up.
💡
Not sure? Start with IEPRequest the eval in writing. You can step down to a 504 — you can't easily go up.

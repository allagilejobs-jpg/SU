IEP vs 504 Plan

Which does your autistic child need?

IEP
✓ Specialized instruction
✓ Individualized goals
✓ Progress monitoring
✓ Related services (OT, Speech)
✓ Legal protections under IDEA
✓ Annual review required
✓ For kids who need specialized teaching
504
✓ Accommodations only
✓ No specialized instruction
✓ Less paperwork
✓ Faster to set up
✓ Civil rights protection
✓ Less formal review
✓ For kids who just need accommodations
💡 Not sure? Start with IEP request. You can always move to 504 later.

📋 Newly Diagnosed

The First 48 Hours
After Diagnosis

Stop the frantic 2am Googling. Start here.

Hour 1-6
Feel Your Feelings
Grief, relief, fear, hope — all valid. Don't make any big decisions yet. Just breathe.
Hour 6-12
Call 3 People
1) Your pediatrician for referrals. 2) Your insurance for coverage info. 3) Your school district about early intervention.
Hour 12-24
Get on Waitlists
Speech, OT, ABA — whatever was recommended. Don't wait until you "know more." Waitlists are 6-12 months.
Hour 24-36
Find Your People
Join one Facebook group or follow autism parent accounts. You need people who GET IT.
Hour 36-48
Take Care of YOU
Sleep. Eat. Hug your kid. The marathon starts now. You need to pace yourself.
🤍 Your child is still the same kid they were yesterday.
The diagnosis is information, not a tragedy.

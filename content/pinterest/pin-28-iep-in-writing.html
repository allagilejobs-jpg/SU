⚖️ IEP Advocacy

Always Request IEP
Meetings in WRITING

📧 Email Creates a Paper Trail
Schools MUST respond within a legal timeframe. This protects YOU.
🔍 Why It Matters
✓ Verbal requests can be "forgotten" or denied
✓ Written requests start the legal clock (usually 30 days)
✓ Creates documentation if you need to escalate
✓ Shows you know your rights (schools take you seriously)
✅ What to Include
1. Your child's name and grade
2. Specific request (meeting, evaluation, etc.)
3. Date of your request
4. Request written confirmation of receipt
📝 Copy This Template
"Dear [Special Ed Coordinator], I am writing to formally request an IEP meeting for my child, [Name], in [Grade]. I would like to discuss [topic]. Please confirm receipt of this request and provide available dates. Thank you, [Your Name] [Date]"

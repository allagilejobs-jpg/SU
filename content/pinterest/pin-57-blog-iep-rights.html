IEP Rights Schools Won't Tell You

Know your rights under IDEA

Most schools won't volunteer these. But IDEA guarantees them.
📝
You Don't Have to Sign Same Day
Take the IEP home, review it, consult an advocate. Never feel pressured.
🎯
You Can Request ANY Meeting
You don't have to wait for annual review. Request one in writing anytime.
📊
You Can Request Independent Evaluation
Disagree with school's eval? You can request an IEE at district expense.
🏠
"We Don't Have That" Isn't Legal
If your child needs it, they must provide it or pay for it elsewhere.
⚖️
You Can Bring Anyone to Meetings
Advocate, lawyer, friend, therapist. You choose your team.
📋
Get Everything in Writing
Verbal promises mean nothing. If it's not in the IEP, it doesn't exist.

🧑‍🎓 Teen Years

Autism in Teens

Puberty + autism = a whole new journey

🧬
Body Changes Hit Different
Sensory issues intensify. Hygiene routines need explicit teaching. Growth spurts disrupt body awareness.
👥
Social Gap Widens
Peers advance socially faster. Subtle social rules become more complex. Loneliness often peaks.
🎭
Identity Questions Intensify
"Who am I?" meets "Why am I different?" Self-acceptance becomes crucial.
🏫
School Demands Increase
More teachers, more transitions, less structure. Executive function is tested daily.
💔
Mental Health Risks Rise
Anxiety and depression rates spike. Burnout becomes real. Monitor closely.
How to Support Your Teen
✓ Respect their autonomy
✓ Explicit puberty education
✓ Connect with autistic peers
✓ Teach self-advocacy
✓ Maintain safe spaces
✓ Plan for transitions
💾 Save for the teen years ahead

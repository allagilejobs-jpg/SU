Workplace Accommodations for Autistic Adults

What parents need to know for their child's future

⚖️ ADA Protections
✓ Autism is a protected disability under ADA
✓ Employers must provide "reasonable accommodations"
✓ Can't be fired solely for being autistic
🏢 Common Workplace Accommodations
→ Noise-canceling headphones or quiet workspace
→ Written instructions instead of verbal
→ Flexible scheduling or remote work
→ Clear, explicit expectations
→ Reduced fluorescent lighting
🆓 JAN (Job Accommodation Network)
Free government service most families don't know exists!
📝 How to Request Accommodations
1. Document the request in writing
2. Explain how autism affects job tasks
3. Suggest specific accommodations
4. Know they can't ask about diagnosis details

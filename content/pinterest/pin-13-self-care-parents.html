💜 For Parents

Self-Care for
Autism Parents

Not bubble baths. Real strategies.

⚠️ THE REALITY
You're running on empty, appointments fill your calendar, and "me time" feels impossible. We get it.
✅ MICRO SELF-CARE THAT ACTUALLY WORKS
☕
Hot Coffee Rule
Finish ONE hot drink before it gets cold. That's a win.
🚗
Parking Lot Pause
Sit in car for 5 min before going inside. Breathe.
🎧
Sensory Reset
Noise-canceling headphones. Even for 10 minutes.
📱
Phone Boundaries
No screens first 30 min awake. Or during meals.
🌙
Sleep When Possible
Everything else can wait. Rest is productive.
🚶
Movement Snacks
5-min walk. Stretch at red lights. Small counts.
👥 FIND YOUR PEOPLE
Facebook groups Local support groups Therapy for YOU Autism parent friends
💜 You can't pour from an empty cup. Your needs matter too.

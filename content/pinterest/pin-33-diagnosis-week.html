📋 Newly Diagnosed

Just Got a Diagnosis?
This Week's Checklist

5 steps that actually help

1
Take a Breath
This is a lot. You don't have to figure everything out today. Your child is still the same amazing kid.
2
Get on Waitlists NOW
Speech, OT, ABA — whatever was recommended. Waitlists are 6-12 months. You can always decline later.
3
Contact Your School District
Request an evaluation for services. Early intervention and IEPs are your right. Put it in writing.
4
Find Your People
Join a Facebook group, follow autism parent accounts. You need people who understand.
5
Take Care of YOU
This is a marathon, not a sprint. Sleep. Eat. Ask for help. You can't pour from an empty cup.
🤍 The diagnosis is information. Not a limit. Not a tragedy. Just the start of understanding.

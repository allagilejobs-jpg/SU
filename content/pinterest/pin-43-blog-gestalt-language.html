What Is a Gestalt Language Processor?

And why it changed everything for my son

"Your child scripts full sentences from Bluey but can't say 'milk'?"
That's gestalt language processing.
1
Echolalia — Repeating whole phrases from TV, songs, or people
2
Mixing — Combining parts of different scripts together
3
Single words — Isolating individual words from chunks
4
Novel phrases — Creating new 2-word combinations
5
Sentences — Building original sentences with grammar
6
Complex — Full, flexible spontaneous language

Marriage & Autism
Parenting

The strain is real. So is the hope.

Divorce rates for autism parents are higher than average. But it doesn't have to be your story.
😔 Why It's Hard
• All energy goes to the kids - nothing left for each other
• Different coping styles create conflict
• One parent often becomes the "expert"
• Date nights feel impossible
• Grief timelines don't match
💚 What Helps
✓ Schedule 15 min daily check-ins
✓ Share the "expert" role
✓ Respite care for date nights
✓ Couples therapy (autism-informed)
✓ Individual therapy too
✓ Lower expectations, not standards
✓ Tag team parenting
✓ Celebrate small wins together
💕 Your relationship matters too.
Taking care of your marriage IS taking care of your kids.

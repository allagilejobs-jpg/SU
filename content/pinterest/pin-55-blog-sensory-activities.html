7 Sensory-Friendly Rainy Day Activities

OT-approved, parent-tested

1
🫧 Sensory Bin Exploration
Rice, beans, kinetic sand with hidden toys to find
Needs: bin + filler + small toys
2
🎵 Indoor Obstacle Course
Pillows to jump on, blanket tunnels, couch cushion climbing
Needs: stuff you already have
3
🎨 Shaving Cream Art
On a tray or in the bathtub for easy cleanup
Needs: shaving cream + food coloring
4
💧 Water Transfer Play
Cups, funnels, basters between containers
Needs: kitchen items + towels
5
🧁 Calming Playdough
Homemade with calming scents (lavender)
Needs: flour, salt, cream of tartar, oil
6
🏠 Blanket Fort + Books
Create a cozy, low-stimulation retreat space
Needs: blankets + fairy lights + books
7
🎧 Dance Party / Freeze Dance
Movement + music + impulse control practice
Needs: music + space

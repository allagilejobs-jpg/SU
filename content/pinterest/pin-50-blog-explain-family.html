How to Explain Autism to Family Who Don't Get It

Scripts, strategies, and boundary-setting

📝 Script for Grandparents
"Their brain processes the world differently. When they cover their ears, it's not rudeness — sounds actually hurt. When they need routine, it's not stubbornness — it's how they feel safe."
🚫 When They Say Harmful Things
→ "They just need discipline" — No, they need understanding
→ "They'll grow out of it" — Autism is lifelong
→ "They seem fine to me" — You see 2 hours, not the meltdown after
✅ Setting Boundaries
✓ "I need you to trust me on how to parent my child"
✓ "If you can't respect our accommodations, visits will be shorter"
✓ "I'd love to share resources that explain it better than I can"
💡 Key Phrase
"You don't have to fully understand autism to fully accept my child."

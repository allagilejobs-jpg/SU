🌪️ Know The Difference

Meltdown vs Tantrum

They look similar but need completely different responses

😤
TANTRUM
🎯 Goal-oriented behavior
👀 Checks for audience
🛑 Can stop if need is met
🧠 In control of actions
📍 Happens in specific contexts
🌪️
MELTDOWN
⚡ Overwhelm response
🧠 Neurological crisis
🚫 Cannot just "stop"
❌ NOT manipulation
🔋 Needs recovery time after
✅ DURING A MELTDOWN — DO:
✓ Stay calm yourself
✓ Reduce sensory input
✓ Give space if safe
✓ Use few, simple words
✓ Just BE there
✓ Give recovery time after
💙 Meltdowns aren't misbehavior. They're a sign your child needs SUPPORT.

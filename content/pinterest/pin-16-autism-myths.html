🚫 Myth Buster

5 Autism Myths
That Need to Die

You've probably believed at least one

✕ "Vaccines cause autism"
✓ Exposed as fraud in 1998. Dozens of studies with millions of children prove no link.
✕ "Autistic people lack empathy"
✓ Many feel emotions MORE intensely. They may express it differently, not feel it less.
✕ "You don't look autistic"
✓ There is no "look." Autism is neurological, not physical. This dismisses real struggles.
✕ "Only boys are autistic"
✓ Girls mask better and get missed. Real ratio may be 1:1, not 4:1.
✕ "They'll grow out of it"
✓ Autism is lifelong. They learn to adapt, not "recover." Acceptance > cure mentality.

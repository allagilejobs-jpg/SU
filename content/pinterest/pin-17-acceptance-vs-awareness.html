💡 Language Matters

Acceptance vs Awareness

Why the autism community moved on

💡
Awareness
• "Know autism exists"
• Focus on deficits and challenges
• Talks ABOUT autistic people
• Often leads to pity
• "Light it up blue" (not preferred)
• Goal: Recognize autism
💛
Acceptance
✓ "Embrace autistic people as they are"
✓ Celebrates differences and strengths
✓ Listens TO autistic voices
✓ Leads to inclusion and support
✓ "Light it up gold" (community preferred)
✓ Goal: Include and accommodate

The Shift That Matters

Awareness says "I see you." Acceptance says "I welcome you."

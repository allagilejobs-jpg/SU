Therapy Waitlist? What to Do While You Wait

Don't waste the waiting time

Stuck on a 6-month waitlist?
Here's what you can do RIGHT NOW.
1
Get on Multiple Lists
Call EVERY provider in your area today. The more lists, the faster you'll get a spot.
2
Contact Early Intervention
Under 3? State EI is FREE and often has shorter waits than private therapy.
3
Learn to Support at Home
YouTube SLPs, Hanen Programs, sensory activities. Every interaction is therapy.
4
Check for Cancellations
Call weekly. Ask to be on cancellation list. Be ready to drop everything.
5
Consider Telehealth
Online therapy often has shorter waits. Great for speech and parent coaching.
6
Document Everything
Keep a log of behaviors, challenges, progress. Therapists will thank you.

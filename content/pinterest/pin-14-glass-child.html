Glass Child Syndrome

Is your neurotypical child becoming invisible?
The siblings who fade into the background.

Warning Signs
😶
Minimizes Their Own Needs
"It's fine, I don't need anything" becomes their default response
🏆
Overachieves to Get Noticed
Perfectionism driven by wanting any attention at all
😔
Hides Emotions to Not "Burden" You
They see you're exhausted and don't want to add more
🤐
Stops Sharing About Their Day
Believes their stories aren't important enough
How to Help
✓ Scheduled 1-on-1 time weekly
✓ Ask about THEIR day first
✓ Validate their frustrations
✓ Let them be a kid, not helper
✓ Consider sibling support groups
✓ Celebrate their wins loudly

Your First IEP Meeting

A checklist for parents

📋 What to Bring
✓ Copy of all evaluations
✓ List of your concerns (written)
✓ Notebook and pen for notes
✓ Someone for support (advocate, spouse, friend)
✓ Water — meetings run long
💬 What to Say
→ "I'd like that in writing, please"
→ "Can you show me the data supporting that?"
→ "I need time to review this before signing"
→ "What does that look like in the classroom?"
⚖️ Know Your Rights
★ You are an EQUAL member of the IEP team
★ You don't have to sign anything same day
★ "We don't do that here" is not a legal reason
★ Everything must be documented in writing
⚠️ Record the meeting (check your state's consent laws)

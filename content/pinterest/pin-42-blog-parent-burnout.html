Autism Parent Burnout Is Real

It doesn't mean you're failing. It means you're human.

😫
It's 2am and No One Is Awake
Your child won't sleep, no therapist is answering, and you're running on empty. That's burnout.
⚡
Why It Hits Autism Parents Harder
Constant advocacy, sensory vigilance, therapy schedules, insurance fights. The load never stops.
🚨
Warning Signs You're Burned Out
Irritability, detachment, physical exhaustion, dreading each day. These aren't character flaws.
💚
Where to Find Real Help
Respite care, parent support groups, therapy for YOU, and asking for help without guilt.

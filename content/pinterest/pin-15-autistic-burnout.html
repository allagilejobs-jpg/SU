🔥 Know the Signs

Autistic Burnout

Some experience their first burnout at age 6

👶 Signs in Kids
📉 Skills regression (loses abilities they had)
😫 More frequent, intense meltdowns
🏫 School refusal or avoidance
🛋️ Increased need for alone time
😴 Exhaustion after "normal" activities
🙅 Refusing previously enjoyed things
🧑 Signs in Adults
🧠 Executive function collapse
💔 Emotional exhaustion, numbness
🎭 Can't mask anymore (and don't care)
🏠 Isolation, withdrawing from everyone
🤕 Physical symptoms (headaches, illness)
❓ Questioning identity and self-worth
⚠️ Recovery Takes Time
Burnout isn't fixed with a weekend off. It can take months. Reduce demands, increase support, and be patient.

💬 For Friends & Family

What TO Say to
Autism Parents

Share this with the people in your life

✕ Don't Say
"I'm so sorry"
Implies autism is a tragedy
✓ Say This
"Thanks for sharing with me"
Acknowledges trust, no judgment
✕ Don't Say
"They'll grow out of it"
Autism is lifelong; dismisses reality
✓ Say This
"How can I support you?"
Opens door for actual help
✕ Don't Say
"They don't look autistic"
There is no "look"; dismisses struggles
✓ Say This
"Tell me about them"
Shows interest in the whole child
✕ Don't Say
"God only gives special kids..."
Can feel dismissive of real struggles
✓ Say This
"You're doing a great job"
Simple validation goes SO far
✕ Don't Say
"Have you tried [random cure]?"
We've researched more than you know
✓ Say This
"Can I bring you dinner/coffee?"
Practical help > unsolicited advice
💡 When in doubt: Listen more, advise less.

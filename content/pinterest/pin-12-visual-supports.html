Tools to reduce anxiety and increase independence

Visual Schedules
Show the day's plan with pictures or words
✓ Reduces "what's next?" anxiety
✓ Helps with transitions
⏰
Visual Timers
Make time visible with countdown timers
✓ Shows "how long" concretely
✓ Eases transition warnings
1️⃣
First-Then Boards
"First [task], then [reward]"
✓ Motivates completion
✓ Breaks down tasks
🎯
Choice Boards
Offer 2-4 options visually
✓ Gives control
✓ Reduces overwhelm
📖
Social Stories
Prepare for new situations with story format
✓ Explains expectations
✓ Reduces fear of unknown
🃏
Break Cards
Non-verbal way to request a break
✓ Self-advocacy tool
✓ Prevents meltdowns

Keep visuals consistent across home, school, and therapy. Laminate for durability!

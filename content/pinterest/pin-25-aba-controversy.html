The ABA Controversy

What parents need to know

ABA (Applied Behavior Analysis) is the most common autism therapy.
It's also the most debated. Here's both sides so YOU can decide.
🚩 Concerns Raised
• Can teach masking over authenticity
• Historical use of punishment
• 40 hrs/week too intensive for young kids
• May target harmless stims
• Many autistic adults report trauma
• Focus on compliance over wellbeing
✓ In Defense
• Modern ABA has evolved significantly
• Can teach functional skills
• Play-based approaches exist
• Good BCBAs prioritize child's needs
• Evidence-based outcomes
• Quality varies by provider
❓ Questions to Ask Providers
→ Do you allow stimming?
→ How do you handle "no"?
→ Is it play-based?
→ What does success look like?
→ Can parents observe?
→ Hours per week recommended?
💡 You know your child best. Trust your gut. There are alternatives if ABA isn't right for you.

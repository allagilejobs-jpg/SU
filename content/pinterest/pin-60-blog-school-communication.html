📝 Documentation Strategies
✓ Always communicate in writing (email > phone)
✓ Follow up verbal conversations with email summary
✓ Keep a dated folder of all correspondence
✓ CC yourself on every email you send
💡 Key Phrases That Work
→ "Per our conversation on [date]..."
→ "Please confirm receipt of this email"
→ "I am requesting this in writing under IDEA"
→ "What is the timeline for [action]?"
📧 Sample: "Dear [Teacher], I wanted to follow up on our conversation about [issue]. As we discussed, [summary]. Please let me know if I've misunderstood anything. Thank you, [Your name]"

🚫 Myth Busted

Autistic Kids Feel
Emotions Deeply

✕ The Myth
"Autistic people don't have emotions or empathy"
✓ The Truth
They often feel MORE intensely. They just express it differently.
🌊
Emotional flooding is real. They feel so much it can be overwhelming and shut them down.
🎭
Expression ≠ Experience. A flat face doesn't mean a flat heart. They may feel deeply but not show it "typically."
🤝
Hyper-empathy is common. Many autistic people absorb others' emotions intensely, sometimes painfully.
⏰
Delayed processing happens. They might cry about something hours or days later once they've processed it.
"Just because I don't express it the way you expect doesn't mean I don't feel it."

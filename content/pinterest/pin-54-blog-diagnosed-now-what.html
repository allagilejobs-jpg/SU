Just Diagnosed? Now What?

A parent's step-by-step guide

"Your child has autism." Here's what to do next.
1
Breathe. Process. Feel.
Give yourself time. This diagnosis doesn't change who your child is.
2
Get the Report
Get written diagnosis. You'll need it for services, school, insurance.
3
Contact Early Intervention or School
Under 3: state EI program (free). 3+: request school evaluation in writing.
4
Check Insurance Coverage
Most states require ABA coverage. Call and document everything.
5
Get on Waitlists NOW
Speech, OT, specialists — lists are months long. Get on all of them today.
6
Find Your People
Facebook groups, local parent meetups. You need support, not just information.
7
Take Care of YOU
You can't pour from empty. Therapy, respite, rest are not selfish.

Sleep Strategies for Autistic Kids

Evidence-based approaches that work

🧠 Why Sleep Is Harder
Sensory sensitivities, anxiety, circadian rhythm differences, and difficulty "shutting off" the brain all contribute to sleep challenges in autistic children.
🌙
Create a Sensory-Friendly Bedroom
Blackout curtains, white noise, weighted blanket, cool temperature, remove visual clutter.
📋
Visual Bedtime Routine
Same order every night, visual schedule, predictable = calming. No surprises before bed.
📱
Screen Cutoff Time
No screens 1-2 hours before bed. Blue light disrupts melatonin production.
💊
Beyond Melatonin
Melatonin helps some kids. But it's not the only option — talk to your pediatrician.

Evidence-based approaches for autistic kids who can't shut their brain off

1
Build a sensory-friendly bedroom Blackout curtains. White noise. Weighted blanket. Cool temp. Remove visual clutter.
2
Same routine, every night Visual schedule, same order, no surprises. Predictability = the calm before sleep.
3
No screens 1–2 hours before bed Blue light disrupts melatonin. Books, audiobooks, dim lights instead.
4
Melatonin isn't the only option It helps some kids. Talk to your pediatrician about other options first.

Building Friendships

Friendship looks different. That's okay.

Autistic kids want connection.
They just connect differently.
🌟 What Autistic Friendship Looks Like
💫 Parallel play at any age (side by side, not face to face)
🎮 Bonding over shared interests, not small talk
📅 Less frequent hangouts but deeper connections
🌍 Online friendships count (sometimes preferred)
✓ Do This
✓ Interest-based activities
✓ Structured playdates
✓ Shorter visits first
✓ Find neurodivergent peers
✓ Prep social scripts
✕ Avoid This
✕ Forcing eye contact
✕ "Just go play!"
✕ Surprise social events
✕ Comparing to siblings
✕ Large group activities
💡 Finding Their People
🎯 Special interest clubs (LEGO, coding, art, gaming)
🏕️ Autism-friendly camps and programs
📱 Online communities with shared interests

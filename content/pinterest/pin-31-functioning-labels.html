🚫 Language Matters

"High-Functioning" Is
NOT a Compliment

Why functioning labels harm everyone

Functioning labels describe how autism LOOKS to others, not how it FEELS to live with.
"High Support Needs" People...
✕ Get underestimated
✕ Assumed to have no inner world
✕ Denied autonomy and choices
✕ Spoken about, not spoken to
"Low Support Needs" People...
✕ Get denied services and help
✕ "You don't look autistic"
✕ Struggles are minimized
✕ Expected to "just try harder"
✓ What to Say Instead
✓ "High/low support needs"
✓ Describe specific strengths
✓ Describe specific challenges
✓ Ask the person what they prefer

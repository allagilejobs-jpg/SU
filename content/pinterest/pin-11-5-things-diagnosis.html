💙 Newly Diagnosed

5 Things I Wish
I Knew After Diagnosis

What no one tells you on day one

1
Therapy waitlists are LONG
Get on multiple waitlists NOW, even before you're sure.
2
Your pediatrician isn't the expert
Find specialists. Join parent groups. Real knowledge is in the trenches.
3
IEPs are negotiable
Everything can be changed. You have more power than they want you to know.
4
Other autism parents become your village
Friends who "don't get it" will fade. That's okay.
5
Your child is still amazing
The diagnosis didn't change them. It gave you a roadmap.

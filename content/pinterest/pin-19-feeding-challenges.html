🍽️ Food & Sensory

Feeding Challenges
in Autistic Kids

It's not defiance. It's sensory.

🧠 Why It Happens
• Texture sensitivity (slimy, crunchy, mixed)
• Smell overwhelm before food reaches mouth
• Color and appearance matter intensely
• Brand/packaging changes = different food
• Interoception issues (not recognizing hunger)
✓ What Helps
✓ Same plate, same utensils
✓ Foods not touching
✓ Consistent brands
✓ No pressure, no bribes
✓ Exposure without eating
✓ Feeding therapy (SOS)
✕ What to Avoid
✕ "Just try one bite"
✕ Hiding foods in others
✕ Making it a battle
✕ Comparing to siblings
✕ Withholding safe foods
✕ Shaming eating habits
🍟 Chicken nuggets are keeping your kid alive.
That's not failure. That's meeting them where they are.

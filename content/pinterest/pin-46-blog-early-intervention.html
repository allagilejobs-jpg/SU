Autism Early Intervention

What parents need to know

Early intervention can reshape your child's developmental trajectory
🧠
Why Early Matters
Brain plasticity is highest in the first 3 years. Early support builds stronger neural pathways.
🏃
Which Services First
Speech therapy and OT are usually top priorities. ABA depends on your child's needs.
📋
How to Access Services
Contact your state's early intervention program (free for under 3) or school district (3+).
⏰
What If There's a Waitlist?
Get on multiple lists NOW. Do home activities while waiting. Every interaction is therapy.

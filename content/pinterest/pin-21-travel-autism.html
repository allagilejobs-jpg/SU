✈️ Travel Guide

Traveling with an Autistic Child

Yes, you can do this. Here's how.

🛫 Airport Hacks
✓ Request TSA Cares or airline disability assistance
✓ Pre-board when offered (game changer)
✓ Download airport maps, practice the route visually
✓ Noise-canceling headphones are non-negotiable
🏨 Hotel Tips
✓ Call ahead about quiet rooms (away from elevators)
✓ Bring familiar bedding or pillowcase from home
✓ Request mini fridge for safe foods
✓ Pack a nightlight and white noise machine
🎒 Packing Essentials
✓ Safe snacks (airports are sensory nightmares)
✓ Favorite comfort items/stim toys
✓ Tablet loaded with familiar shows
✓ Visual schedule of the trip
🗓️ Prep Strategies
✓ Social stories about travel weeks before
✓ Build in MORE downtime than you think
✓ Practice "what if" scenarios
✓ Have an exit plan for every activity
💡 PRO TIP
Theme parks offer disability passes. Ask about sensory-friendly hours. Many have quiet rooms.
💾 Save for your next trip!

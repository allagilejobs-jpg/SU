How to Handle Public Meltdowns

Without shame

"Your child is on the floor of Target and everyone is staring."
✓ In the Moment: DO
✓ Get low, speak quietly
✓ Reduce sensory input (lights, sounds)
✓ Offer comfort items or stims
✓ Wait it out safely — don't rush
✓ Move to a quieter space if possible
✕ In the Moment: DON'T
✕ Try to reason or lecture
✕ Raise your voice
✕ Physically force compliance
✕ Threaten or punish
👀 For Judgmental Strangers
→ "My child is autistic. We're okay."
→ Carry cards explaining autism
→ Ignore them — your child comes first
→ Remember: their opinion doesn't matter

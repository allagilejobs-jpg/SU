Self-Care for Autism Parents

Strategies that actually work

"This advice was clearly written by someone who's never spent an hour on hold with insurance."
🛑
Not Bubble Baths
Real self-care is setting boundaries, asking for help, saying no, and getting actual rest.
🆘
Ask for (and Accept) Help
Respite care, family help, paid babysitters who get it. You cannot do this alone forever.
🧠
Therapy for YOU
A therapist who understands autism parenting. Your mental health matters.
⏰
Micro-Moments
5 minutes of silence. Hot coffee. A locked bathroom. Small wins count.

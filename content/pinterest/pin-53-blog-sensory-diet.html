A Beginner's Guide to Sensory Diets

No, it's not about food

A sensory diet is a schedule of activities that helps your child stay regulated
🧠 What Is a Sensory Diet?
→ Planned sensory activities throughout the day
→ Prevents meltdowns by meeting sensory needs proactively
→ Customized to YOUR child's sensory profile
→ Created with OT guidance (but you can start at home)
🎯 Sample Activities by Need
🏃 Jumping (heavy work)
🤗 Tight hugs (deep pressure)
🎧 Music (auditory)
💧 Water play (tactile)
🔄 Spinning (vestibular)
🧸 Chewing (oral)
⏰ When to Use It
✓ Before challenging activities (grocery store, appointments)
✓ At regular intervals throughout the day
✓ When you notice dysregulation signs

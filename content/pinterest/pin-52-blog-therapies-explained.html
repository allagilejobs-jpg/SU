Autism Therapies Explained

ABA, OT, Speech & More — Plain language guide

🗣️ Speech Therapy
Communication skills, AAC, social language, articulation. Often first recommended.
🤸 Occupational Therapy (OT)
Sensory processing, fine motor, daily living skills, self-regulation.
🎯 ABA Therapy
Behavior-based learning. Controversial but common. Ask about play-based approaches.
🏃 Physical Therapy
Gross motor, coordination, balance, body awareness.
🧠 Developmental (DIR/Floortime)
Relationship-based, follows child's lead, builds emotional connections.
👥 Social Skills Groups
Peer interaction, social rules, friendship skills in group setting.

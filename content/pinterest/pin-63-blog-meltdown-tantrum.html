🚨 Meltdown vs tantrum

Meltdown isn't a tantrum

✕ The Confusion
"A meltdown is just a bigger tantrum."
Treating a meltdown like a tantrum makes it worse — and tells your child their brain is bad. The difference matters.
VS
✓ The Truth
One is a brain overload. The other is a goal.
🧠
Meltdown = neurologicalNo goal. Can't be reasoned with. Happens with or without an audience.
🎯
Tantrum = goal-orientedWants something. Checks for an audience. Stops when goal is met or given up.
🔧
Different responsesMeltdown: safety + reduce stimuli + wait. Tantrum: stay calm + hold the boundary.
"Your child isn't giving you a hard time. They're having a hard time." — every veteran autism parent

Meltdown vs Tantrum

Why it matters

"Your child isn't giving you a hard time. They're having a hard time."
Meltdown
→ Neurological overwhelm
→ No goal or demand
→ Can't be reasoned with
→ Happens with or without audience
→ Takes time to recover
→ Child is genuinely distressed
→ Response: safety, reduce stimuli, wait
Tantrum
→ Goal-oriented behavior
→ Wants something specific
→ Responds to negotiation
→ Checks if someone is watching
→ Stops when goal is met/given up
→ More in control
→ Response: stay calm, hold boundaries

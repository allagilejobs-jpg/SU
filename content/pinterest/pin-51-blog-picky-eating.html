Autism & Picky Eating

Strategies beyond "just try it"

It's not defiance. It's sensory.
🧠
Why It Happens
Texture sensitivity, smell overwhelm, color/appearance issues, need for sameness and predictability.
❌
What Doesn't Work
Power struggles, forcing bites, hiding foods, comparing to other kids, withholding safe foods.
✅
What Actually Helps
Food chaining, same brands/plates, exposure without pressure, letting them explore with senses first.
🍟
Remember
Chicken nuggets are keeping your kid alive. That's not failure — that's meeting them where they are.

Supporting Siblings

Helping neurotypical kids understand autism

"Siblings notice more than you think."

How to Explain Autism
"Their brain works differently. Some things are harder, some are easier. We all have different needs."
😔
Feelings They Might Hide
Embarrassment, jealousy, guilt for feeling jealous, worry about the future, loneliness.
⏰
Make Them Feel Seen
Scheduled 1-on-1 time, attend THEIR activities, validate their frustrations without guilt.
🌟
No Child Should Be Invisible
They're not "the easy one" — they have their own needs that matter equally.

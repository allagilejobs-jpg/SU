🚽 No Pressure Guide

Potty Training
an Autistic Child

A Different Timeline — And That's OK

🧠 WHY IT TAKES LONGER
• Interoception challenges (sensing "I need to go")
• Transition anxiety from diapers
• Sensory issues (cold seat, flushing sounds)
• Routine disruption is hard
✅ READINESS SIGNS TO LOOK FOR
✓ Awareness of wet/dirty diaper
✓ Predictable bowel times
✓ Can stay dry for 1-2 hours
✓ Shows interest in bathroom
💡 AUTISM-FRIENDLY STRATEGIES
📋 Visual schedule of each step
⏰ Timed toileting (every 30-60 min)
🎧 Address sensory issues (headphones, warm seat)
🎁 Immediate rewards using special interest
📱 Social stories about using the potty
🧘 ZERO pressure. No punishment for accidents.
📅 REMEMBER
Many aren't fully trained until 5-8+ Regression happens Take breaks if distressed
💙 Your child WILL get there. Don't let anyone rush you.

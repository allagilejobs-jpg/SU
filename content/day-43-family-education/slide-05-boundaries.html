Protecting Your Child
When to Set Limits
Your child's wellbeing comes first. It's okay to limit contact when:
⚠️ They refuse to follow your child's accommodation needs
⚠️ Comments or behavior are harmful to your child's self-esteem
⚠️ They undermine your parenting decisions repeatedly
⚠️ Visits consistently result in meltdowns or regression
⚠️ They show no willingness to learn or change over time
💚 Remember: boundaries protect everyone, including the relationship

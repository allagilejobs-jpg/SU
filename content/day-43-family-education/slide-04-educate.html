Practical Steps
How to Educate
Meet them where they are. Education takes time:
📚
Share resources: Send articles, books, or videos. Let them learn at their pace.
👥
Invite them to therapy: Seeing an OT or speech session can be eye-opening.
📝
Create a "cheat sheet": Write what helps and what doesn't for your specific child.
❤️
Lead with love: Assume positive intent. Most want to help but don't know how.
🚪
Set boundaries: It's okay to limit contact with those who refuse to learn.
⏰
Be patient: It took you time too. Give them grace to catch up.

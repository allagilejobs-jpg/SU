Why It's Hard
Extended family often struggles to understand autism because:
📺
Outdated information: Their knowledge comes from old stereotypes or media portrayals
👁️
"But they look fine!": Invisible disabilities are hard to grasp without education
🛡️
Denial or grief: Accepting the diagnosis can be their own process
📖
Generational gaps: "Kids were just kids" mentality from older generations
💬
Unsolicited advice: They want to help but don't know how

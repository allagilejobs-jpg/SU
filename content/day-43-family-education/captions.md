## 📸 Instagram Caption

👨‍👩‍👧 EDUCATING EXTENDED FAMILY about autism

When grandparents, aunts, and uncles just don't get it. Here's how to bridge the gap ⬇️

Why it's hard:
📺 Outdated information and stereotypes
👁️ "But they look fine!" mindset
🛡️ Their own denial or grief process
📖 Generational gaps in understanding
💬 Unsolicited advice with good intentions

What to say when they don't understand:

"They need more discipline" →
"Their brain processes differently. What looks like defiance is often overwhelm."

"They don't look autistic" →
"Autism is a spectrum. Every autistic person is different."

"We didn't have this in my day" →
"We just didn't have a name for it."

How to educate:
📚 Share resources at their pace
👥 Invite them to therapy sessions
📝 Create a "cheat sheet" for your child
❤️ Lead with love, assume positive intent
🚪 Set boundaries when needed
⏰ Be patient with their learning

When to set limits:
⚠️ They refuse to follow accommodations
⚠️ Their behavior harms your child's self-esteem
⚠️ They repeatedly undermine your parenting
⚠️ Visits consistently cause meltdowns

Your child comes first. Always.

How did you educate your extended family? 👇

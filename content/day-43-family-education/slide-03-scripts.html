What to Say
"They just need more discipline."
Try: "Their brain processes the world differently. What looks like defiance is often overwhelm or communication."
"They don't look autistic."
Try: "Autism is a spectrum. Every autistic person is different, just like every person is different."
"We didn't have autism in my day."
Try: "We just didn't have a name for it. Many adults are only now getting diagnosed."
"Have you tried...?"
Try: "I appreciate you wanting to help. Right now what helps most is accepting them as they are."

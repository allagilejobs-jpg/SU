## Instagram Caption

👫💔 GLASS CHILD REVISITED: Don't Let Siblings Disappear

When one child needs more attention, the other can become invisible.

WHAT IS A "GLASS CHILD"?
A sibling who becomes invisible because their brother/sister requires so much attention. You can see right through them.

Signs your child may be fading:
🤫 They stop asking for help
😊 They become "the easy one"
🎭 They hide their own struggles
👤 They fade at family events
😔 They internalize that their needs matter less

THE LONG-TERM IMPACT:
😰 Anxiety and hypervigilance
🤷 Low self-worth
🧹 People-pleasing patterns
😶 Difficulty expressing needs
😤 Hidden resentment
🏆 Overachievement/perfectionism

HOW TO HELP YOUR GLASS CHILD:
1. Schedule 1-on-1 time (even 15 minutes matters!)
2. Ask about THEIR day
3. Validate their feelings
4. Give space to be "not okay"
5. Celebrate their wins loudly

THE CONVERSATION TO HAVE:
"I know your sibling needs a lot of attention. I want you to know that I see you too. You are just as important. Your needs matter just as much."

It's not about equal time. It's about making sure every child feels equally loved, valued, and visible.

How do you balance sibling needs? Share your tips! 👇

How to Help Your Glass Child
1
Schedule 1-on-1 Time
Even 15 minutes of dedicated attention matters. Put it on the calendar. Protect it.
2
Ask About THEIR Day
Make sure conversations aren't always about their sibling. Their life matters too.
3
Validate Their Feelings
"It's okay to feel frustrated. You can love your sibling AND feel this way."
4
Give Them Space to Be "Not Okay"
They don't always have to be the easy one. Let them struggle too.
5
Celebrate Their Wins
Attend their games, praise their art, notice their achievements loudly.

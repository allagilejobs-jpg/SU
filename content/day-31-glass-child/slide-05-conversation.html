What To Say
The Conversation to Have
Have an honest conversation with your neurotypical child. Here's how it might sound:
Acknowledge
"I know your sibling needs a lot of attention. I want you to know that I see you too."
Validate
"It's okay to feel frustrated, jealous, or sad sometimes. Those feelings make sense."
Affirm
"You are just as important. Your needs matter just as much."
Commit
"I'm going to make sure we have special time together, just for you."
Invite
"You can always tell me how you're feeling. I want to hear it."

What Is a "Glass Child"?
A glass child is a sibling who becomes "invisible" because their brother or sister requires so much attention. You can see right through them.
  • 🤫 They stop asking for help because they know you're overwhelmed
  • 😊 They become "the easy one" to avoid adding stress
  • 🎭 They hide their own struggles to not burden you
  • 👤 They fade into the background at family events
  • 😔 They internalize that their needs matter less
The truth: This isn't about blame. Parents of special needs kids are doing their best with limited resources. But awareness is the first step to change.

## 📸 Instagram Reels Caption

😴💤 SLEEP ISSUES in Autism: What Actually Helps

Melatonin isn't the only answer ⬇️

📊 80% of autistic kids have sleep problems
📊 They get 2+ hours LESS sleep than neurotypical peers

Why sleep is hard:
🧠 Different melatonin production
⚡ Sensory sensitivities at bedtime
😰 Anxiety and racing thoughts
🔄 Difficulty with transitions

Environment tips:
🌑 Complete darkness (blackout curtains)
🔇 White noise machine
❄️ Cool room (65-68°F)
🛏️ Weighted blanket
📵 No screens 1 hour before bed

Create a visual routine:
1️⃣ Bath/shower
2️⃣ Pajamas on
3️⃣ Brush teeth
4️⃣ Read 2 books
5️⃣ Goodnight hugs
6️⃣ Lights out

Better sleep = better days for everyone 🌙

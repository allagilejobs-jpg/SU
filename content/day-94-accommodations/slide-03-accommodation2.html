Sensory Breaks + Safe Person
These two accommodations prevent more meltdowns than anything else.
Sensory Breaks

Ask for scheduled breaks every 45-60 minutes. Specify what the break looks like: walk, fidget, quiet corner. Don't leave it vague.

Safe Person

Identify one adult in the building your child trusts. Make sure your child knows their name, where to find them, and that it's okay to go to them.

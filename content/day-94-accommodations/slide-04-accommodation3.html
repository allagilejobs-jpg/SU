Communication + Transitions
Small systems that make a massive difference in your child's day.
Communication System

A card they can place on their desk, a hand signal, or a lanyard system. Your child shouldn't have to verbally ask for help in front of the whole class.

Transition Warnings

Sudden changes are a top meltdown trigger. Get 5-min and 2-min warnings written into the IEP. Visual timers on the desk help too.

How To Help
Supporting Your Autistic Teen
1
Talk About Puberty Early
Use clear, concrete language. Visual guides and social stories help immensely.
2
Create Safety to Unmask
Home should be a place where masking isn't required. Accept stims, needs, and downtime.
3
Foster Independence Gradually
Break skills into steps. Practice life skills with patience and repetition.
4
Connect Them With Peers
Interest-based groups, online communities, autism meetups help them find their people.
5
Validate Their Experience
Teen struggles are real. Listen without minimizing. Be their safe person.

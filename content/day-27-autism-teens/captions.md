## Instagram Caption

🧒➡️🧑 AUTISM IN TEENS: What changes and what stays

Puberty + autism = a whole new journey. Here's what you need to know:

PHYSICAL CHANGES + AUTISM:
🌡️ Sensory overload intensifies (hormones amplify sensitivity)
🎢 Emotional regulation gets harder
👕 Body changes feel jarring
😰 Anxiety often spikes

SOCIAL CHALLENGES:
👥 Peer pressure and complex social rules
💔 Feeling different becomes more pronounced
🎭 Masking exhaustion and burnout risk
💕 Navigating dating and romance
📱 Social media dynamics
🎯 Identity formation ("Who am I?")

HOW TO SUPPORT YOUR AUTISTIC TEEN:
1. Talk about puberty early with clear, concrete language
2. Create safety to unmask at home
3. Foster independence gradually
4. Connect them with interest-based peer groups
5. Validate their experience - be their safe person

THE GOOD NEWS:
🎯 Deep focus becomes powerful skills
💡 Original thinking leads to creativity
🤝 Authentic connections with "their people"
⚖️ Strong values and sense of justice

Many autistic teens who struggle go on to THRIVE as adults. The teen years are hard, but they're not forever.

Parents of autistic teens: what's your biggest challenge? 👇

💾 Save this for the teen years ahead

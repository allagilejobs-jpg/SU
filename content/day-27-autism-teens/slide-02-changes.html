Physical Changes + Autism
Puberty brings major sensory and emotional shifts. For autistic teens, these can feel overwhelming:
  • 🌡️ Sensory overload intensifies - hormones amplify sensitivity to textures, sounds, smells
  • 🎢 Emotional regulation harder - already challenging, now with hormonal mood swings
  • 👕 Body changes feel jarring - new sensations, new clothes needed, body awareness shifts
  • 😰 Anxiety may spike - uncertainty about what's "normal" and what's not
Remember: All of this is temporary. With support, autistic teens can navigate puberty successfully.

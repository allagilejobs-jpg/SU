## 📸 Instagram Reels Caption

🧩💪 OT DEEP DIVE: What It Really Is

Occupational Therapy is way more than just "playing" ⬇️

WHAT IS OT?
OTs help kids do the "occupations" of childhood: playing, learning, self-care, and socializing. Think of it as helping the WHOLE child function in DAILY life.

WHAT OT HELPS WITH:
🧠 Sensory processing
✋ Fine motor skills
🍴 Feeding/eating challenges
👕 Self-care (dressing, hygiene)
✏️ Handwriting
😌 Emotional regulation

WHAT SESSIONS LOOK LIKE:
(Yes, it looks like play. That's the point!)
🎢 Swings and movement activities
🧊 Sensory bins and textures
🧩 Puzzles and fine motor games
🎨 Art and crafts
🏃 Obstacle courses
🧘 Calming strategies

FINDING THE RIGHT OT:
✓ Look for pediatric specialization
✓ Ask about autism experience
✓ Check insurance coverage first
✓ Request a sensory gym tour
✓ Trust your gut on fit!

Good OT can be life-changing for autistic kids 💙

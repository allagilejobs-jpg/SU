📋 IEP Tips
IEP Meeting Checklist
Screenshot this for your next meeting
Before
Request draft IEP 3 days early (your right!)
Write down your top 3 priorities
Bring someone with you if possible
During
Ask them to explain anything unclear
Take notes or record (check state laws)
You do NOT have to sign same-day
If They Say No
Ask for the denial in writing
Request the specific policy they're citing
"I'd like to revisit this at a follow-up"
You are the expert on your child 💪

## 📸 Instagram Reels Caption

🌱💪 BUILDING INDEPENDENCE

Small steps today, big skills for life ⬇️

THE MINDSET SHIFT:
Independence isn't about doing everything alone. It's about having the skills to navigate life with support when needed.

Progress over perfection. Every small step counts.

AREAS TO BUILD:
🚿 Self-care routines
🏠 Household tasks
💰 Money basics
🚌 Transportation
🗣️ Self-advocacy
📱 Tech/safety skills

HOW TO BUILD SKILLS:
✓ Break tasks into tiny steps
✓ Use visual checklists
✓ Practice in low-stakes settings
✓ Fade prompts gradually
✓ Celebrate every win
✓ Allow time and patience

START SMALL TODAY (Pick ONE):
🪥 Brushing teeth independently
👕 Choosing their own clothes
🍽️ Putting dishes in the sink
🎒 Packing their own bag
📞 Ordering food (with support)

Every skill learned is a step toward their future 🌟

What skill are you working on? Share your wins below! 🎉

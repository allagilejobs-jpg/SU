SENSORY AVOIDING
🛡️
Overwhelmed by Input
The brain registers too much sensory information, so it tries to escape or reduce it.
🙉 Covers ears: Distressed by everyday sounds — dryers, toilets, crowds
👕 Clothing battles: Tags, seams, certain fabrics feel painful
🍽️ Limited foods: Textures, smells, or temperatures trigger refusal
😰 Avoids crowds: Malls, parties, assemblies feel unbearable
💡 Light sensitivity: Fluorescent lights, sunlight, or screens cause distress

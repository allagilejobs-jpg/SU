Many Kids Do BOTH
It's not either/or. A child can seek input in one area and avoid it in another — sometimes on the same day.
Seeking
⚡ Craves deep pressure hugs
⚡ Loves loud music
⚡ Jumps and crashes constantly
⚡ Seeks strong flavors
Avoiding
🛡️ Can't stand light touch
🛡️ Covers ears in crowds
🛡️ Hates certain textures
🛡️ Avoids bright lights
This is why one-size-fits-all sensory strategies don't work. An OT can create a personalized sensory diet for YOUR child.

How to Advocate for ESY
1
Request ESY in writing BEFORE the IEP meeting
2
Document regression after every break (weekends, holidays, sick days)
3
Ask for data — request the school's regression/recoupment records
4
Know that ESY is NOT limited to academics — it can include speech, OT, behavior
5
If denied, ask for Prior Written Notice and consider filing a complaint

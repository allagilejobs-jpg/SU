## 📸 Instagram Reels Caption

😰💙 AUTISM & ANXIETY: The Hidden Connection

Up to 84% of autistic people experience anxiety. Here's what you need to know ⬇️

WHY IT'S SO COMMON:
🌍 World feels unpredictable
👥 Social situations are exhausting  
🔊 Sensory overload is constant
🎭 Masking takes huge energy
❓ Change = threat response

SIGNS (often looks different!):
• Increased stimming
• More rigid/controlling behavior
• Meltdowns over "small" things
• Avoiding activities they used to enjoy
• Sleep problems
• Physical complaints (tummy aches)

WHAT ACTUALLY HELPS:
📋 Predictable routines
⏰ Transition warnings
🏠 Safe space to decompress
🧸 Comfort objects
💬 Visual supports
🧘 Sensory regulation tools

Try 5-4-3-2-1 grounding adapted for sensory-sensitive kids!

Anxiety is treatable. You don't have to figure this out alone. 💙

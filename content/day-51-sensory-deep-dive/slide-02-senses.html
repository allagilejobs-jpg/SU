Beyond Five Senses
The 8 Sensory Systems
We actually have 8 senses, not 5. Here they all are:
👁️
Visual
Light, colors, movement, patterns
👂
Auditory
Sounds, pitch, volume, filtering
🤲
Tactile
Touch, texture, temperature, pressure
👃
Olfactory
Smells and scent sensitivity
👅
Gustatory
Taste and food textures
🧘
Vestibular
Balance, motion, spatial awareness
💪
Proprioception
Body position and pressure needs
🫀
Interoception
Internal body signals (hunger, pain)

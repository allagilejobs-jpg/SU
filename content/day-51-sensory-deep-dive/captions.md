## Instagram Caption

👂 Sensory processing: the thing that affects EVERYTHING

Most people talk about the 5 senses. But we actually have 8:

1. Visual (light, colors, patterns)
2. Auditory (sounds, filtering noise)
3. Tactile (touch, texture, temperature)
4. Olfactory (smell sensitivity)
5. Gustatory (taste and food textures)
6. Vestibular (balance and motion)
7. Proprioception (body awareness)
8. Interoception (internal signals)

Autistic people can be:

⬆️ HYPERSENSITIVE (over-responsive)
- Lights feel blinding
- Background noise is overwhelming
- Clothing tags feel like torture
- Certain textures cause physical distress

⬇️ HYPOSENSITIVE (under-responsive)
- Craving deep pressure and tight hugs
- Needing to spin, jump, crash
- Seeking strong flavors
- Not noticing pain or injuries

And here's the key: You can be BOTH. Hypersensitive to sound but hyposensitive to proprioception. It varies by sense and by day.

Tools that help:
🎧 Noise-canceling headphones
🕶️ Tinted glasses for light
🧸 Fidgets and stim toys
🛏️ Weighted blankets
🏠 Quiet decompression spaces

Sensory needs aren't preferences. They're how the nervous system processes the world.

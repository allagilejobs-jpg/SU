Why Nature Helps 🌿
Research shows nature exposure benefits everyone, but for autistic individuals, the effects can be particularly powerful:
  • 🧠 Reduces cortisol - Stress hormones drop significantly after just 20 minutes outdoors
  • 🔊 Natural soundscape - Wind, water, and birds provide gentler auditory input than urban noise
  • 👁️ Soft visual input - Greens and natural patterns are calming to the visual system
  • 🏃 Proprioceptive regulation - Uneven terrain, climbing, and movement naturally regulate the body
Key insight: Nature doesn't demand social performance. It's one of the few places to just BE.

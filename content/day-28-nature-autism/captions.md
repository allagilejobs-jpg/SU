## Instagram Caption

🌍🌿 NATURE AND AUTISM: Sensory Benefits of the Outdoors

Happy Earth Day! Free therapy is right outside your door.

WHY NATURE HELPS AUTISTIC INDIVIDUALS:
🧠 Reduces cortisol (stress hormones drop after 20 mins)
🔊 Natural soundscape (gentler than urban noise)
👁️ Soft visual input (greens and natural patterns are calming)
🏃 Proprioceptive regulation (uneven terrain, climbing)

💡 Nature doesn't demand social performance. It's one of the few places to just BE.

NATURE HITS EVERY SENSE:
👁️ Visual: Greens, blues, natural fractals
👂 Auditory: Water, leaves, birdsong
✋ Tactile: Sand, grass, bark, water
👃 Olfactory: Fresh air, flowers, earth
🏃 Vestibular: Hills, swings, climbing
💪 Proprioceptive: Carrying, digging, hiking

ACTIVITIES THAT WORK:
🚶 Nature walks (even 10 minutes!)
🔍 Nature scavenger hunts
🌱 Gardening
💧 Water play
🪨 Rock and stick collecting
📷 Nature photography

TIPS FOR SUCCESS:
- Start small (5 minutes counts!)
- Go during off-peak times
- Bring sensory supports
- Follow their lead
- Have an exit plan

It won't be Instagram-perfect. That's okay. Any time outside is beneficial!

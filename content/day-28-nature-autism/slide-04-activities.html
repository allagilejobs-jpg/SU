Try These
Outdoor Activities That Work
🚶
Nature Walks
Start small. Even 10 minutes counts. Let them set the pace.
🔍
Nature Scavenger Hunts
Find 5 green things, 3 smooth rocks, 1 bird. Structure + nature = win.
🌱
Gardening
Digging, planting, watering. Predictable, routine-based, sensory-rich.
💧
Water Play
Streams, puddles, sprinklers. Many autistic kids find water incredibly regulating.
🪨
Rock and Stick Collecting
Honoring special interests while outdoors. Let them collect!
📷
Nature Photography
A camera or phone gives purpose. Looking closely at nature.

Sensory Benefits
Nature Hits Every Sense
👁️
Visual
Greens and blues are calming. Natural patterns (fractals) reduce stress more than straight lines.
👂
Auditory
Water sounds, rustling leaves, birdsong. Predictable, gentle, non-jarring sounds.
✋
Tactile
Sand, grass, bark, water. Varied textures for sensory seekers. Space to avoid for avoiders.
👃
Olfactory
Fresh air, flowers, earth after rain. Natural scents without artificial overwhelm.
🏃
Vestibular
Hills, swings, climbing. Natural opportunities for movement and balance.
💪
Proprioceptive
Carrying sticks, digging, hiking. Heavy work happens naturally outdoors.

Tips for Success 🌳
Start Small
5 minutes in the backyard counts. Build up gradually. Don't force hour-long hikes.
Go During Off-Peak Times
Avoid crowded parks. Early mornings or weekdays are often quieter and calmer.
Bring Sensory Supports
Sunglasses, headphones, fidgets, sunscreen they'll tolerate. Prepare for success.
Follow Their Lead
If they want to stare at ants for 30 minutes, that's nature time. Let it happen.
Have an Exit Plan
Know where the car is. Have a signal for "I need to leave." Reduce pressure.
💡 It won't be Instagram-perfect. That's okay. Any time outside is beneficial time.

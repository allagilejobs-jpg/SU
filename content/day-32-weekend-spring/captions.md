🌸☀️ AUTISM-FRIENDLY SPRING WEEKENDS + Products That Actually Work

Weekend activities that won't overwhelm your kiddo:

🌿 Nature walks (early morning = fewer crowds)
💧 Water play at home (sensory win!)
🎨 Outdoor art projects
🏕️ Backyard camping (bathroom nearby!)

Products we actually use:
🎧 Noise-reducing headphones
🧸 Portable fidgets
🎒 Comfort kit bag (always packed)
🌡️ Weighted lap pad
🕶️ Sunglasses for visual overwhelm
📱 AAC app/device

Pro tips:
1. Preview the day with visuals
2. Build in downtime between activities
3. Know where quiet spaces are
4. Go during off-hours when possible

Reality check: Some weekends, staying home in pajamas IS the activity. Rest is productive too.

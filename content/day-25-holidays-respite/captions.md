## Instagram Caption

🎉 Making events autism-friendly + why respite MATTERS

Family gatherings don't have to be disasters. Here's how to survive them:

Before the event:
📸 Preview the location with photos/videos
📝 Create a social story about what will happen
🎒 Pack the kit: headphones, fidgets, safe snacks, comfort items
⏰ Set a time limit in advance
🚪 Scout quiet spaces before you arrive
👋 Have an exit code word
👨‍👩‍👧 Brief family on dos and don'ts
🍕 Bring safe foods

During the event:
⏱️ Take regulation breaks every 30 min
🛡️ Be the buffer with overwhelming relatives
🎯 Let them parallel play - it's valid!
📱 Screens are regulation tools (ignore the judgment)
🚗 Leave BEFORE the meltdown, not after

Now let's talk RESPITE:

Taking a break isn't abandonment. It's survival.

"You can't pour from an empty cup."

Benefits:
🔋 Prevents caregiver burnout
❤️ Strengthens your relationship
👫 Time for other relationships
🧘 Restores mental health

Where to find respite:
🏛️ State/county Medicaid waiver programs
🏢 The Arc, Easter Seals, Autism Society
👨‍👩‍👧 Train trusted family/friends
⛪ Faith community programs
🏕️ Specialized camps

You DESERVE a break. Taking care of yourself = showing up long-term.

What's YOUR survival tip for family events? 👇

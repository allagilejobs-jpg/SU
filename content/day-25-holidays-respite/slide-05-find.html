Where to Look
Finding Respite Care
🏛️ State/County Programs
Many states offer funded respite through Medicaid waivers. Ask your case manager.
🏢 Disability Organizations
The Arc, Easter Seals, Autism Society chapters often provide or connect to respite.
👨‍👩‍👧 Family and Friends
Train a trusted person. Even 2 hours makes a difference.
⛪ Faith Communities
Some churches offer "parents night out" programs with trained volunteers.
🏕️ Specialized Camps
Day camps or overnight camps for kids with disabilities = respite for you.
Start small. A few hours. Build up as your child adjusts to new caregivers.

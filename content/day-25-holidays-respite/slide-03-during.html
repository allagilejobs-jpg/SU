During the Event
Survival Strategies
⏱️
Take Regulation Breaks
Every 30 min, check in. Step outside, use the quiet space, reset.
🛡️
Be the Buffer
Intercept overwhelming relatives. "They need space right now."
🎯
Let Them Parallel Play
Playing alone near others is valid. Don't force interaction.
📱
Screens Are Okay
Tablet time at a party = regulation tool. Ignore the judgment.
🚗
Leave When You Need To
Before the meltdown, not after. Your child's needs > others' expectations.

Before the Event
Event Prep Tips
📸
Preview the Location
Show photos/videos of where you're going. Visit ahead if possible.
📝
Social Story
Write a simple story about what will happen, who will be there.
🎒
Pack the Kit
Headphones, fidgets, safe snacks, comfort item, noise/escape plan.
⏰
Set a Time Limit
Decide in advance how long you'll stay. Leave before meltdown point.
🚪
Scout Quiet Spaces
Know where the calm zones are before you arrive.
👋
Prep the Exit
Have a code word for "I need to leave." No explanations needed.
👨‍👩‍👧
Brief Family
Tell relatives what helps. Give them a quick "dos and don'ts."
🍕
Bring Safe Foods
Don't count on event food. Bring what your child will actually eat.

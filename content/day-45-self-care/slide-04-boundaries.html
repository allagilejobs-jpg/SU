Boundaries as Self-Care
Sometimes self-care means saying no. Try these:
📵
Phone-free zones: Don't answer every text immediately. The world can wait.
🚫
Skip events: You don't owe anyone your presence when you're running on empty.
🗣️
Stop explaining: "That doesn't work for our family" is a complete sentence.
📋
Delegate: Let go of perfectionism. Good enough IS good enough.
🛑
Protect energy: Limit time with people who drain you.

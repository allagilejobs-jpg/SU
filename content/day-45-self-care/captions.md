## Instagram Caption

🧘 Self-care for autism parents: NOT what you think

Let's be honest. You're exhausted. You're touched out. And someone telling you to "take a bubble bath" makes you want to scream.

Here's what ACTUALLY works:

The hard truth:
❌ Bubble baths don't fix burnout
❌ "Me time" often doesn't exist
❌ Traditional advice wasn't made for us

What really helps:

⏱️ MICRO-MOMENTS
- 2 minutes of deep breathing
- Hiding in the bathroom (we see you)
- One hot cup of coffee, finished

🚧 BOUNDARIES
- "I can't do that right now"
- Screen time isn't failure
- Saying no to one more therapy appointment

👥 SUPPORT SYSTEMS
- Online groups who GET IT
- Scheduled respite (not just emergencies)
- Therapy for YOU, not just your kid

🗣️ Asking for help
- Specific requests work better than hints
- "Can you watch them for 30 minutes?"
- Let people help imperfectly

Remember: You can't pour from an empty cup.

Taking care of yourself isn't selfish. It's survival strategy.

## 📸 Instagram Reels Caption

🧠✨ 5 SENSORY HACKS that actually work!

Meltdown prevention starts here ⬇️

1️⃣ Noise-canceling headphones: Keep a pair in your bag at ALL times

2️⃣ Sunglasses indoors: Fluorescent lights are brutal. Comfort > "looking normal"

3️⃣ Fidget tools everywhere: Car, backpack, pockets. Always within reach

4️⃣ Visual timers: Makes time visible. "5 more minutes" finally makes sense!

5️⃣ Calm-down corner: A sensory-safe space at home. Let THEM design it

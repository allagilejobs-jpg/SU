Initial Meeting Tips
☐
Bring your "Getting to Know My Child" letter (see Day 88)
☐
Share a copy of the IEP summary — not the full document, just the highlights
☐
Ask: "What's the best way to communicate?" Email? App? Notes in the folder?
☐
Ask: "What do you need from me?" Partnership is a two-way street.
☐
Keep it short and positive. 15-20 minutes max. Save heavy topics for later.

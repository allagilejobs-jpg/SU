The First Approach
💛
Assume good intent. Most teachers want to help. They may just not know how yet.
⭐
Lead with strengths. Tell them what your child is great at before you list the challenges.
📧
Reach out first. Don't wait for back-to-school night. Send a warm email before day one.
🙏
Express gratitude early. "Thank you for taking the time to learn about my child" goes a long way.

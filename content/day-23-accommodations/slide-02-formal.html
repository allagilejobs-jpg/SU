Formal Options
IEP vs. 504 Plan
📋 IEP
Individualized Education Program
Requires disability that impacts learning
Includes specialized instruction
Annual goals and progress tracking
More legal protections
Full evaluation required
📄 504 Plan
Section 504 of Rehabilitation Act
Broader eligibility
Accommodations only (no special ed)
Less paperwork
Faster to get
Still legally binding!
Pro tip: Some kids have BOTH! 504 plans can cover things IEPs don't, like health accommodations.

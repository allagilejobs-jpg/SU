Making It Work
Documentation Tips
1
Put It In Writing
Even informal agreements - email the teacher to confirm. "Per our conversation..."
2
Be Specific
Not "help with transitions" but "5-minute warning before activity changes"
3
Build Relationships First
Teachers who know your child will go further. Collaborate, don't battle.
4
Know Your Rights
IDEA, Section 504, ADA - learn the basics. Knowledge is power.
5
Follow Up
Check in monthly. Accommodations only work if they're actually used.

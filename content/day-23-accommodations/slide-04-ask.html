Hidden Options
Things You Can Ask For
📚 Reduced Homework
Quality over quantity. Ask for modified assignments that show mastery without burnout.
🗓️ Advance Notice of Subs
Text or email when there's a substitute teacher so you can prepare your child.
🧪 Alternative Assessments
Oral tests, projects instead of written exams, portfolio-based grading.
🚌 Transportation Support
Smaller bus, specific seat, headphones allowed, aide support.
🎭 Modified Participation
Alternative to presentations, exemption from certain activities, quiet role options.
Remember: If you don't ask, the answer is always no. Schools often have flexibility they don't advertise.

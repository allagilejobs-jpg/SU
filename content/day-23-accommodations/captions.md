## Instagram Caption

🏫 School accommodations go WAY beyond the IEP

You can ask for more than you think. Here's what most parents don't know:

Formal options:
📋 IEP: Specialized instruction + accommodations + legal protections
📄 504 Plan: Accommodations only but faster, broader eligibility
💡 Pro tip: Some kids have BOTH!

Informal accommodations (no paperwork needed):
🪑 Flexible seating - near door, wobble chair, standing desk
🎧 Sensory tools - headphones, fidgets, sunglasses
⏰ Timing adjustments - early dismissal, movement breaks
📝 Assignment mods - typed not handwritten, verbal answers
🚪 Safe exit plan - signal to leave when overwhelmed
📧 Written instructions and advance notice of changes
🍎 Food flexibility - snacks in class, quiet eating space
👥 Social supports - lunch bunch, buddy system

Things you CAN ask for:
📚 Reduced homework (quality over quantity)
🗓️ Advance notice of substitute teachers
🧪 Alternative assessments - oral tests, projects
🚌 Transportation support
🎭 Modified participation options

Documentation tips:
1️⃣ Put everything in writing
2️⃣ Be specific, not vague
3️⃣ Build relationships first
4️⃣ Know your rights (IDEA, 504, ADA)
5️⃣ Follow up monthly

Accommodations aren't special treatment. They're equal access.

What accommodation changed YOUR child's school life? 👇

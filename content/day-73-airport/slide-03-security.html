Getting Through Security
✓ Tell the TSA officer your child has autism upfront
✓ You can request a private screening if needed
✓ Medical devices, food, and liquids for disability needs are allowed
✓ Let your child keep shoes on if removing them causes distress
✓ Carry a card explaining your child's needs (print or phone)
💡 Download TSA's disability notification card at tsa.gov before your trip.

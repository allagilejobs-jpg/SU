When to Get Help
Talk to a Professional If...
Sometimes environmental changes aren't enough. Seek help if you notice:
⏰
Takes more than 1 hour to fall asleep most nights
🌙
Wakes multiple times and can't self-soothe back to sleep
😤
Daytime behavior significantly affected by lack of sleep
😰
Sleep anxiety causing extreme bedtime distress
👩‍⚕️ Who Can Help
• Pediatrician (rule out medical issues)
• Sleep specialist or neurologist
• Occupational therapist (sensory strategies)
• Behavioral therapist (sleep training modifications)

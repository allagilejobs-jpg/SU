Environment Tips
Set Up the Space
🌑
Total Darkness
Blackout curtains, cover all LED lights, no nightlights (unless needed for safety)
🔇
Sound Control
White noise machine or fan to mask unpredictable sounds
🛏️
Sensory Bedding
Weighted blanket, specific fabric textures, seamless sheets
🌡️
Temperature
Cool room (65-68°F) helps trigger sleep. Use breathable fabrics.
🧸
Comfort Items
Same blanket, pillow, or stuffed animal every night for security
📱
No Screens
Remove screens 1-2 hours before bed. Blue light disrupts melatonin.

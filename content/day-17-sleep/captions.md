## 📸 Instagram Caption

🌙 SLEEP ISSUES in autism: what actually helps

Melatonin isn't the only answer. Here's what else works ⬇️

📊 50-80% of autistic kids have sleep problems
📊 They get 2+ hours LESS sleep than neurotypical peers

Why sleep is so hard:
🧠 Different melatonin production
⚡ Hard to wind down an active nervous system
🔊 Sensory sensitivities (sheets, sounds, light)
😰 Co-occurring anxiety

Environment fixes:
🌑 Total darkness (blackout curtains, cover LEDs)
🔇 White noise to mask unpredictable sounds
🛏️ Weighted blanket, specific textures
🌡️ Cool room (65-68°F)
📱 No screens 1-2 hours before bed

Routine matters:
✅ Same time every night
✅ Visual schedule of bedtime steps
✅ Predictable sequence (bath, PJs, book, bed)
✅ Same comfort items every night

When to get help:
⚠️ Takes 1+ hour to fall asleep most nights
⚠️ Multiple wakings with no self-soothing
⚠️ Daytime behavior significantly affected

What helps YOUR child sleep? Share your tips! 👇

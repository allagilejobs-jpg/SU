## Instagram Caption

🔍💡 LATE DIAGNOSIS: The Adult Autism Journey

It's never too late to understand yourself.

WHY SO MANY ARE DIAGNOSED LATER:
📚 Outdated criteria (built around young white boys)
🎭 High masking (learned to "pass" at great cost)
👩 Gender bias (women significantly underdiagnosed)
🏥 Misdiagnosis (labeled anxiety, depression instead)

450% increase in adult autism diagnoses in recent years. You're not alone.

SIGNS IN ADULTS:
😮‍💨 Exhaustion after social events
🎭 Feeling like you're "performing" socially
🌡️ Strong sensory sensitivities
🔥 Deep, consuming interests
📋 Need for routines and structure
👽 Lifelong feeling of being "different"
🤯 Difficulty with unexpected changes
💬 Taking things literally, missing cues

THE EMOTIONAL JOURNEY:
- Relief: "There's a reason I struggled"
- Grief: "What if I'd known sooner?"
- Anger: "Why didn't anyone catch this?"
- Identity shift: "Who am I really?"
- Acceptance: "I can live authentically now"

WHAT COMES NEXT:
1. Learn about autism (from autistic creators)
2. Find community (late-diagnosed adults)
3. Accommodate yourself (honor your needs)
4. Consider neurodiversity-affirming therapy
5. Be patient with identity reconstruction

Late-diagnosed? Tell me your "aha" moment 💡👇

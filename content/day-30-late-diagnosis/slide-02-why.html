Why So Many Are Diagnosed Later
Many autistic adults spent decades without understanding why they felt different. Here's why it happens:
  • 📚 Outdated criteria - Diagnostic tools were built around young white boys. Many didn't fit the stereotype.
  • 🎭 High masking - Learned to "pass" as neurotypical at great personal cost.
  • 👩 Gender bias - Women and girls were (and still are) significantly underdiagnosed.
  • 🏥 Misdiagnosis - Often labeled with anxiety, depression, or personality disorders instead.

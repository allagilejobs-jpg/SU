Success Tips
Making It Work
📅 Start now. It's never too early (or too late) to begin building skills.
🎯 One skill at a time. Focus on one skill until it's solid before adding more.
⏰ Build in time. Independence takes longer. Plan for it. Don't rush.
🎉 Celebrate small wins. Every step forward counts. Acknowledge progress.
💪 Expect setbacks. Regression is normal. Keep going anyway.
🤝 Include them. Let your child have input on what skills they want to learn.
The goal isn't perfection. It's progress. Their best is good enough.

Strategies
How to Teach
🔗
Task Analysis
Break big tasks into tiny steps. Teach one step at a time. Chain them together.
📸
Visual Supports
Picture schedules, video modeling, checklists. Show, don't just tell.
🔄
Backward Chaining
Do most of the task, let them complete the last step. Build backward to full independence.
👐
Prompt Fading
Start with hand-over-hand, fade to gestures, then verbal cues, then independence.
🎯
Practice in Context
Practice money at real stores, not just worksheets. Real-world learning sticks.

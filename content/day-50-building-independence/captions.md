## Instagram Caption

🌱 Building independence in autistic kids: It's a marathon, not a sprint

Independence looks different for everyone. Here's how to build it, step by step.

THE MINDSET SHIFT:
❌ "They'll never be able to..." → ✅ "What support do they need?"
❌ "I'll just do it faster" → ✅ "I'll build in extra time"
❌ "Independence means alone" → ✅ "Independence means less support"
❌ "They should be doing this by now" → ✅ "What's their next step?"

SKILLS TO BUILD:
🚿 Self-care: hygiene, dressing, grooming
🍳 Food: snacks, microwave, simple cooking
🏠 Home: laundry, cleaning, organizing
💰 Money: recognizing coins, paying for items
🚌 Community: transit, ordering, shopping
⏰ Time: schedules, timers, routines
📱 Technology: phone, apps, safety
🗣️ Communication: asking for help, self-advocacy

HOW TO TEACH:
🔗 Task analysis: Break big tasks into tiny steps
📸 Visual supports: Picture schedules, video modeling
🔄 Backward chaining: Let them complete last step, build backward
👐 Prompt fading: Hand-over-hand → gestures → verbal → independent
🎯 Practice in context: Real stores, real situations

TIPS FOR SUCCESS:
📅 Start now (it's never too early or late)
🎯 One skill at a time
⏰ Build in extra time
🎉 Celebrate small wins
💪 Expect setbacks
🤝 Include them in choosing goals

Their best is good enough. Progress, not perfection.

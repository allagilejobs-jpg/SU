## 📸 Instagram Reels Caption

🤝🏠 BUILDING YOUR SUPPORT SYSTEM

You were never meant to do this alone ⬇️

4 TYPES OF SUPPORT YOU NEED:
💬 Emotional - People who listen without judgment
🛠️ Practical - Help with tasks, errands, childcare
📚 Informational - Guidance on resources, rights, systems
🤗 Social - Community who truly "gets it"

WHERE TO FIND YOUR PEOPLE:
🏥 Your child's therapy clinic
🏫 School special ed community
💻 Online support groups
🏛️ Local autism organizations
⛪ Faith communities with inclusion
🏋️ Special needs sports/activities

THE HARDEST PART:
Asking for help feels vulnerable. But people WANT to help. They just don't know how.

💡 Be specific: "Can you pick up groceries?" works better than "I need help"

Community isn't optional. It's how we survive and thrive. 💙

Who's in your village? Tag them below! 👇

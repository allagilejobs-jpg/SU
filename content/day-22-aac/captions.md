## Instagram Caption

💬 Not speaking ≠ not communicating

AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication) opens doors for nonverbal and minimally verbal children.

The facts:
📊 25-30% of autistic individuals are nonspeaking or minimally verbal
📊 100% of people have the right to communicate

AAC options:
📱 High-tech: Tablets with speech apps (Proloquo2Go, TouchChat)
📋 Low-tech: Picture boards, PECS, communication books
🤟 Sign language: ASL or personalized gestures
✋ Unaided: Body language, pointing, facial expressions
✏️ Writing/typing: Letter boards, keyboards
🎨 Core word systems: 50 words = 80% of communication!

How to support AAC success:
🗣️ Model it yourself - show them how!
🌍 Keep AAC available 24/7
⏳ Give 10-15 seconds wait time
🎉 Honor ALL communication attempts
🚫 Never withhold AAC to "make them talk"

Busting myths:
✅ AAC does NOT prevent speech - research shows it helps!
✅ No prerequisites - communication is a right
✅ You're never "too young" for AAC
✅ AAC can be used alongside speech

Communication is a right. Not a privilege. Not something to earn.

Does your family use AAC? Share your experience 👇

💾 Save this for families who need it

How to Help
Supporting AAC Success
🗣️
Model, Model, Model
Use AAC yourself when talking to your child. Show them how it works!
🌍
AAC Everywhere
Keep it accessible always - not just for "practice time." Communication is 24/7.
⏳
Wait Time is Magic
Give 10-15 seconds after asking. Processing takes time.
🎉
Honor ALL Communication
Respond to gestures, sounds, and behaviors. They're talking to you.
🚫
Don't Require Speech
Never withhold AAC to "make them talk." That's like taking away someone's wheelchair.

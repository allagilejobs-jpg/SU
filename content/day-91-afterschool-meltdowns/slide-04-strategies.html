In-the-Moment Strategies
🤫
Don't Ask Questions
"How was school?" can wait. Greet them with quiet presence.
🍎
Offer a Snack
Low blood sugar + overload = explosion. Food first, talk later.
🧘
Create a Landing Pad
A quiet, low-demand space they can decompress in. No screens yet.
⏰
Give Them Time
30-60 minutes of zero demands. Let their nervous system reset.

Prevention for Tomorrow
You can't eliminate meltdowns, but you can reduce the load.
🎒
Pack a sensory kit in their backpack (fidget, gum, noise-canceling earbuds)
📋
Request sensory breaks be built into the school day
🏠
Minimize after-school demands. No homework at the door.
💬
Communicate with the teacher about what the school day looks like
😴
Protect sleep. An overtired kid has zero reserves left to cope.

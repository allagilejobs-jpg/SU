Break It Into Steps
✅ Task analysis: Break every skill into tiny, teachable steps (brushing teeth = 8-10 mini steps)
✅ Visual checklists: Use pictures or icons so they can follow independently
✅ Model first: Show, don't just tell. Do it together before expecting solo
✅ Backward chaining: Teach the last step first so they finish with success
✅ Consistent routine: Same steps, same order, same time every day
✅ Fade prompts gradually: Go from hand-over-hand to gesture to independent

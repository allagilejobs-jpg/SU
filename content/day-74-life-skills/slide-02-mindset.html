The Right Mindset
Independence isn't about perfection. It's about building confidence through supported practice.
💡 Drop the timeline. Your child's pace is the right pace. Comparison steals progress.
🤝 Collaborate, don't command. "Let's try this together" works better than "Do it now."
🎯 Focus on effort, not outcome. Attempting the task IS the win — celebrate it.
🔄 Expect regression. Bad days don't erase good ones. Skills come in waves.

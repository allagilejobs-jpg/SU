Pro Tips
📸 Photo schedules: Take pictures of YOUR child doing each step — personal visuals work best
🔁 Practice when calm: Teach new skills during low-stress times, not during meltdowns
🎉 Natural rewards: Connect tasks to motivators — "After shoes on, we go to the park!"
📝 Track progress: Keep a simple log to see growth when daily life feels stuck
Remember: The goal isn't perfection. It's progress and confidence.

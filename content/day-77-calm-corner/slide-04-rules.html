Rules for the Corner
✅ Always voluntary: Your child chooses to go there — never forced
✅ No time limit: They leave when they feel ready, not on a timer
✅ Parents can use it too: Model self-regulation. "I need my calm corner."
✅ Practice when calm: Explore the corner together during good moments
✅ Respect the space: Don't lecture, question, or follow them in
The calm-down corner teaches: "I have the tools to help myself feel better."

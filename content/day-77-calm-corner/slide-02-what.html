What Is a Calm-Down Corner?
A designated safe space where your child can go to self-regulate when overwhelmed.
🧠 It teaches self-regulation — the ability to recognize and manage big emotions independently.
💜 It's proactive, not reactive. Introduce it during calm times, not during a meltdown.
NOT This
❌ Time-out
❌ Punishment
❌ Isolation
THIS
✅ Safe space
✅ Self-chosen
✅ Regulation tool

Why This Myth Is Harmful
⚠️ Twice-exceptional (2e) kids exist. Gifted AND disabled. They need support for BOTH.
📉 Good grades don't mean access. A child masking all day to "perform" is not thriving.
⚖️ IDEA law is clear: Eligibility is based on disability + need for specially designed instruction, NOT test scores.
🚫 Schools cannot refuse evaluation because a child is "doing fine academically."
If a school says this to you, request the evaluation in writing. They have 60 days to respond. Know your rights.

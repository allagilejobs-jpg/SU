## 📸 Instagram Caption

👧 SIGNS OF AUTISM IN GIRLS that often get missed

Autism doesn't look the same in everyone. Girls are diagnosed 4-5 years later than boys on average.

Here's what to look for ⬇️

Why girls get missed:
📊 Diagnostic criteria were developed studying mostly boys
📊 Girls are better at masking from an early age
📊 The real ratio may be 2:1 or 3:1, not 4:1

Signs that get overlooked:
🎭 Social camouflaging (copies others to fit in)
👯 One intense friendship rather than groups
📚 "Acceptable" special interests (animals, reading, celebrities)
🏠 Holds it together at school, meltdowns at home
😰 Diagnosed with anxiety/depression first

More signs to know:
🪞 Sensory sensitivities (tags, textures, noise)
📝 Extreme perfectionism
🗣️ Advanced vocabulary, prefers adults
🎪 Imaginative play but scripted (re-enacting movies)
😓 Needs lots of alone time to recover

If this sounds familiar:
1. Document what you see (videos help)
2. Find evaluators experienced with girls
3. Trust your gut, get second opinions
4. Start support even before diagnosis

Were you or your daughter diagnosed late? Share your story 👇

💾 Save this for families who need it

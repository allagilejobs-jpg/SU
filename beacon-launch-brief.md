# Beacon Cold Launch Brief — 6 Weeks, Organic-First

**Hand-off to social media session.** Everything below is the source of truth for the 6-week launch plan. Ship week 1 first; report back before starting week 2.

---

## Context

Beacon (`beacon.spectrumunlocked.com`) is an AI companion for autism parents. It is live but has zero public awareness. Parent brand = Spectrum Unlocked (`spectrumunlocked.com`).

Goal: drive public awareness through organic FB + IG for 4 weeks, then $500 paid boost in weeks 5–6. Solo operator. Sustainability matters more than volume.

## Core principle

Earn the audience by naming their pain *before* pitching the product. "Meet our new app!" posts to a zero-follower account convert at zero. First 2 weeks: no Beacon mention. Week 3 is the reveal.

## Channels

FB + IG only. Same content repurposed per platform.

---

## 6-week plan

| Wk | Theme | Posts | Beacon mention? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundation | Profile setup, 3 value posts from existing SU content, follow 50–100 target accounts | No |
| 2 | Name the problem | Burnout post + relatable parent-experience content (see lead asset below) | No |
| 3 | The reveal | Founder story, "Meet Beacon", FAQ carousel, daily IG stories | Yes (primary focus) |
| 4 | Demo | Screen-record reels (IEP prep, 2am), use-case carousels | Yes |
| 5 | Social proof + paid | Testimonials if available, $500 Meta boost on best organic post | Yes |
| 6 | Sustainable rhythm | Lock in 2 FB + 3 IG + 3 reels/wk long-term | 30% of content |

---

## Lead asset (week 2 onward)

New blog post live at spectrumunlocked.com (primary keyword: "autism parent burnout"). Contains ready-to-extract content:
- 5 key takeaways → IG carousel slides
- 4 FAQs → short-form posts
- Bolded definition sentence → hook
- Specific scenes ("it's 2am, your child won't sleep") → reel openers

## Content ratio going forward (week 6+)

60% pure value (no product mention) / 30% Beacon demo or use case / 10% community & behind-the-scenes.

## Post formulas that work for autism-parent content

- "It's 2am and [specific scene]…"
- "If you've [done relatable thing], you're not [judgment they fear]. You're [reframe]."
- "Your kid did X. Here's what actually works."
- "IEP meeting tomorrow? [specific tactic]."
- "The difference between X and Y [that only autism parents see]."

## Avoid

- "Did you know…" (no hook)
- "Here are 5 tips…" (saturated)
- "We're excited to announce…" (zero parent cares)
- Posting just to post. Skip weeks with nothing real to say. Consistency > frequency.

## Paid boost criteria (week 5)

- $20/day × 25 days = $500.
- Target: US, 28–50, autism parents, interests autism awareness + special education + IEP.
- Success: cost per click to Beacon signup < $3. Above $5 = kill ad, try different post.
- Boost top performer from weeks 2–4, not a fresh post. Let Meta's engagement data pick.

## Brand voice guardrails

- No em dashes. Use colons, periods, parentheses, "and".
- Body copy is sentence case.
- Proper nouns capitalized (Beacon, Spectrum Unlocked).
- Never use "we're excited", "disruptive", "game-changing", or any SaaS-marketing language. Parent voice, not product voice.

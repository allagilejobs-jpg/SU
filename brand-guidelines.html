System

Templates & Categories

Understanding how our content system works: templates define structure, categories provide content.

📐 Templates = Visual Structure

Templates define how information is laid out. They're reusable visual frameworks that can hold different content.

Example:
Myth Buster template = Red myth box + Green truth box

🏷️ Categories = Content Topics

Categories define what the content is about. They provide pre-written copy and context for specific topics.

Example:
Sensory category = Headphones, breaks, fidgets content

⭐ Gold Standard Design (Day 4 Reference)

All reels must include these elements:

✨ Decorative Circles

Background depth using ::before and ::after pseudo-elements

.slide::before {
  content: '';
  position: absolute;
  top: -150px;
  right: -150px;
  width: 400px;
  height: 400px;
  background: #E8B86D;
  border-radius: 50%;
  opacity: 0.1;
}

.slide::after {
  content: '';
  position: absolute;
  bottom: -100px;
  left: -100px;
  width: 300px;
  height: 300px;
  background: #4A90A4;
  border-radius: 50%;
  opacity: 0.1;
}

📋 Required Elements

  • Large emoji: 100–150px font-size
  • Number badges: Gold circle, top-right corner
  • Tip boxes: Teal border, rounded corners
  • Gold highlights: Key words in #E8B86D
  • Swipe indicator: "Swipe →" at bottom, teal color
  • Brand handle: @spectrum_unlocked at bottom

🎥 Video Production Methods

🎙️ Method 1: Voiceover + Captions

Narrated video with burned-in subtitles. Best for educational content.

  • ElevenLabs TTS for voiceover
  • Slide timing synced to narration
  • SRT file for captions
  • FFmpeg for compilation

🎵 Method 2: Background Music

Slideshow with music track. Best for emotional/awareness content.

  • Royalty-free music (Pixabay)
  • Even slide timing (4s each)
  • No captions needed
  • FFmpeg for compilation

📄 Full documentation: VIDEO-CREATION.md — Complete FFmpeg commands, music sources, and step-by-step guides.

Logic

Design Decisions

Why each choice matters for accessibility and engagement.

1

Dark Mode Default

Reduces eye strain for evening scrolling. Premium feel. High contrast for accessibility.

2

Gradient Backgrounds

"Spectrum" concept — no flat color. Adds depth, prevents monotony across 60+ graphics.

3

Gold Accent

Symbolizes "unlocking" knowledge. Warm, inviting. Distinct from autism awareness blue.

4

Semantic Colors

Red = myths/warnings. Green = truths. Teal = neutral info. Instant recognition.

5

Large Headlines

62–72px titles readable in Instagram thumbnails. Users decide to swipe based on first slide.

6

Two-Font Limit

Playfair for impact, Poppins for readability. Consistency across 60 days of content.

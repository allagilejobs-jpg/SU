Section 01

Brand Overview

Spectrum Unlocked creates educational content about autism for parents, caregivers, and the neurodivergent community.

🎯 Mission

To provide accurate, neurodiversity-affirming information about autism. We educate, support, and celebrate — moving beyond outdated "awareness" toward true acceptance and understanding.

💙 Core Values

Acceptance over awareness. We center autistic voices, avoid harmful stereotypes, and present autism as a natural neurological variation — not a disease to be cured.

✨ Visual Identity Philosophy

The "Spectrum" in our name inspires our visual language. We use a gradient-based color system that represents the diversity of autism experiences — no single color defines it. Our dark, professional aesthetic conveys credibility while remaining warm and approachable. Gold accents symbolize value and illumination (unlocking knowledge), while teal represents calm, trust, and the neurodiversity movement.

♿ Accessibility Rationale

All text colors meet WCAG AA standards for contrast against our dark backgrounds. White text (#FFFFFF) on #1a1a2e achieves a contrast ratio of 13.5:1. Gold (#E8B86D) on dark backgrounds achieves 7.2:1. We avoid pure black (#000000) as it creates harsh contrast that can cause visual strain, especially for neurodivergent viewers with sensory sensitivities.

Section 03

Typography System

Two carefully paired fonts optimized for social media readability and brand recognition.

🔤 Playfair Display

Role: Display headlines, main titles, quotes
Weights: 600, 700, 800
Style: Serif with high contrast strokes

Why chosen: Adds elegance and authority. The serif style conveys credibility and professionalism — important for educational content. High-contrast letterforms remain readable at large sizes on mobile screens.

🔤 Poppins

Role: Body text, UI, captions, lists
Weights: 400, 500, 600, 700, 800
Style: Geometric sans-serif

Why chosen: Highly legible at all sizes. Geometric forms are friendly and modern. Excellent x-height improves readability on small mobile screens. Google Font ensures consistent rendering across devices.

📱 Social Media Readability Logic

Typography choices are optimized for mobile-first viewing. Instagram carousels are typically viewed on phones at small sizes, so we use:

  • Large headlines (62px+) — Readable even in thumbnail previews
  • High contrast — White/gold on dark backgrounds
  • Short line lengths — Max 800px text width prevents eye strain
  • Generous line-height (1.4–1.6) — Improves readability for neurodivergent audiences
  • Limited font weights — Clear visual hierarchy without confusion

Section 06

Logic & Reasoning

The strategic thinking behind every major design decision — ensuring the editor can consistently recreate and extend 60-day calendar graphics.

1

Dark Mode Default

Dark backgrounds reduce eye strain for viewers scrolling at night (peak Instagram time). Also creates premium, professional feel that differentiates from bright, cluttered competitor content. High contrast ensures accessibility.

2

Gradient Backgrounds

The gradient represents the "spectrum" — no flat, single color. Adds visual depth and prevents monotony across 60+ graphics. The 165° angle (top-left to bottom-right) creates natural reading flow.

3

Gold as Primary Accent

Gold connotes value, warmth, and "unlocking" knowledge. It pops on dark backgrounds without the harshness of pure yellow. Doesn't conflict with autism awareness colors (blue/rainbow) — intentionally distinct.

4

Semantic Color Coding

Red = myths/warnings (universal danger signal). Green = truths/positives (universal success). Teal = neutral info/lists. This instant recognition reduces cognitive load for viewers.

5

Two-Font System

Limiting to 2 fonts (serif display + sans-serif body) creates cohesion across 60 days. More fonts would create inconsistency. Pairing rules: Playfair for headlines/quotes, Poppins for everything else.

6

Oversized Headlines

62–72px titles ensure readability in Instagram's small carousel preview thumbnails. Users decide to swipe based on the first slide — the title must be instantly readable at any size.

7

Consistent Box Styling

All content boxes use the same formula: colored tint (10–15% opacity) + matching border (25–30% opacity) + 16–20px border-radius. This consistency lets viewers instantly recognize "this is a content block."

8

Template Modularity

8 templates cover all content types in the 60-day calendar. Each template is a reusable pattern that can be filled with any content. This ensures visual consistency while allowing content variety.

9

Footer Branding

Handle (@spectrum_unlocked) appears on every slide at 40–50% opacity — visible but not distracting. Slide numbers (1 of 5) help viewers track progress and encourage swiping to completion.

10

Emoji Integration

Native emoji render consistently across all devices and add personality without requiring custom icons. They're universally understood and increase engagement. Placed prominently (80–100px) for visual anchoring.

11

Fabric.js Choice

Fabric.js enables true canvas editing (click-to-edit, drag, resize) while exporting clean PNGs at exact dimensions. HTML/CSS screenshots would have font rendering inconsistencies; Fabric.js ensures pixel-perfect output.

12

Multi-Slide Architecture

Storing each slide as JSON enables saving/loading carousel state, batch exports, and future database storage. Slides are independent but share style variables (accent color, background) for cohesion.

🔗 Consistency Guarantee

Every design decision documented here is implemented in the editor's code. When users create new graphics, they automatically inherit these brand rules through:

  • Template presets — Pre-built layouts with correct spacing, fonts, and colors
  • Color swatches — Only brand-approved colors in the picker
  • Font dropdowns — Limited to Poppins and Playfair Display
  • Size constraints — Canvas locked to 1080px width
  • Default values — All elements start with brand-compliant styling

This means anyone using the editor will produce graphics that match the 60-day calendar — even without reading these guidelines.

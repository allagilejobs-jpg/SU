System

Templates & Categories

Understanding how our content system works: templates define structure, categories provide content.

📐 Templates = Visual Structure

Templates define how information is laid out. They're reusable visual frameworks that can hold different content.

Example:
Myth Buster template = Red myth box + Green truth box

🏷️ Categories = Content Topics

Categories define what the content is about. They provide pre-written copy and context for specific topics.

Example:
Sensory category = Headphones, breaks, fidgets content

Logic

Design Decisions

Why each choice matters for accessibility and engagement.

1

Dark Mode Default

Reduces eye strain for evening scrolling. Premium feel. High contrast for accessibility.

2

Gradient Backgrounds

"Spectrum" concept — no flat color. Adds depth, prevents monotony across 60+ graphics.

3

Gold Accent

Symbolizes "unlocking" knowledge. Warm, inviting. Distinct from autism awareness blue.

4

Semantic Colors

Red = myths/warnings. Green = truths. Teal = neutral info. Instant recognition.

5

Large Headlines

62–72px titles readable in Instagram thumbnails. Users decide to swipe based on first slide.

6

Two-Font Limit

Playfair for impact, Poppins for readability. Consistency across 60 days of content.

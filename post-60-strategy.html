Overview

The Transition

Moving from campaign mode to sustainable content creation.

❌ What We're Leaving Behind

  • Daily posting pressure (2-3 posts/day)
  • Campaign urgency mindset
  • Rigid 60-day schedule
  • Heavy production workload

✅ What We're Building

  • Sustainable 3 posts/week rhythm
  • Content pillars for variety
  • Recurring series for consistency
  • Community-driven content
Phases

Post-Campaign Roadmap

Two phases to transition from campaign to evergreen content.

4

Summer Rhythm

May 25 – August 31, 2026 (14 weeks)

📅 Posting Schedule

  • Monday: Educational content
  • Wednesday: Practical tips
  • Friday: Community/emotional

🎬 Reels

  • 1 reel per week (repurposed from 60-day)
  • Best performers get video treatment
  • New summer-specific reels

📆 Monthly Themes

  • June: Summer Prep & Transitions
  • July: Sensory Summer Activities
  • August: Back-to-School Prep
5

Evergreen Engine

September 2026 onwards

📅 Posting Schedule

  • 2-3 posts per week
  • Recurring series format
  • Flexible based on engagement

🤝 Community Focus

  • Follower Q&A features
  • User story spotlights
  • Collaboration with creators

📈 Growth Strategy

  • Analyze top performers
  • Double down on what works
  • Seasonal content bursts

Series

Recurring Series

Named series build anticipation and habit. Pick 2-3 to start.

Monday
🚫 Myth Monday

Bust one autism myth weekly with facts

Tuesday
💡 Tip Tuesday

One actionable tip in carousel format

Wednesday
🏆 Win Wednesday

Community shares victories big & small

Thursday
🩺 Therapy Thursday

OT, Speech, PT spotlights & tips

Friday
💬 Real Talk Friday

Honest parent moments, no filter

💡 Pro Tip

Don't launch all 5 series at once. Start with 2-3, see what resonates, then add more. Consistency beats variety.

Repurpose

Repurpose the 60 Days

Your existing content is gold. Here's how to maximize it.

🔄

Carousel → Reel

Turn top-performing carousels into video reels with voiceover or music.

📚

Compilation Posts

Combine related days into "Ultimate Guide" mega-carousels.

🔁

Repost Winners

Best performers can be reposted 3-6 months later with minor updates.

📝

Blog/Email Content

Expand carousels into long-form blog posts or email newsletters.

🎙️

Audio Content

Turn educational carousels into podcast episodes or audio tips.

✨

2026 Editions

Update with new info, stats, or perspectives for fresh content.

📊 Top Performers to Repurpose First

After the campaign ends, identify the top 10 posts by saves, shares, and engagement. These are your first candidates for reel conversion and reposting.

Community

Community-Driven Content

Let your followers guide what you create.

📊 Monthly Polls

Use Instagram polls to ask "What topic should I cover next?" Give 4 options, create the winner.

❓ Q&A Carousels

Collect questions via DMs or question stickers. Answer 5-7 in a carousel format.

🌟 Story Features

Feature follower wins, tips, and experiences. Builds community and provides content.

🤝 Creator Collabs

Partner with other autism creators for joint posts, takeovers, or shared series.

Timeline

Implementation Timeline

Week-by-week transition from campaign to sustainable rhythm.

Week 1 (May 25-31)

Transition Week

Rest, analyze 60-day performance, identify top 10 posts. Plan June content. 2 posts only.

Week 2-4 (June)

Summer Prep Theme

3 posts/week rhythm begins. Launch 2 recurring series. First repurposed reel.

Week 5-8 (July)

Sensory Summer Theme

Continue rhythm. Add community Q&A. 1 reel/week. Evaluate what's working.

Week 9-14 (August)

Back-to-School Theme

Ramp up slightly for B2S content burst. Plan fall calendar. Refine series based on data.

September onwards

Evergreen Engine

Settled rhythm. Data-driven content decisions. Community collaborations. Planning for next April.

🎯 Campaign Overview

Beacon (beacon.spectrumunlocked.com) is an AI companion for autism parents. It's live but has zero public awareness. This 6-week plan builds organic audience before paid amplification.

Core Principle: Earn the audience by naming their pain before pitching the product. "Meet our new app!" posts to a zero-follower account convert at zero. First 2 weeks: no Beacon mention. Week 3 is the reveal.

✅ Post Formulas That Work

  • "It's 2am and [specific scene]…"
  • "If you've [done relatable thing], you're not [judgment they fear]. You're [reframe]."
  • "Your kid did X. Here's what actually works."
  • "IEP meeting tomorrow? [specific tactic]."
  • "The difference between X and Y [that only autism parents see]."

❌ Avoid

  • "Did you know…" (no hook)
  • "Here are 5 tips…" (saturated)
  • "We're excited to announce…" (zero parent cares)
  • Posting just to post. Skip weeks with nothing real to say.

IEP meeting tomorrow? Screenshot this. Before you walk in: □ Request the draft IEP 3 days early (your legal right) □ Write down your top 3 priorities □ Bring someone with you if you can In the meeting: □ Ask them to explain anything you don't understand □ Take notes or record (check your state's laws) □ You do NOT have to sign same-day If they say "we can't do that": □ Ask them to put the denial in writing □ Request the specific policy or law they're citing □ Say "I'd like to table this and revisit at a follow-up meeting" You are the expert on your child. Meltdown vs Tantrum

FB + IG | 5-Slide Carousel
Day 6
📁 Instagram Slides 📌 Pinterest Pin
"They're just throwing a tantrum." No. Here's the difference: TANTRUM: • Goal-oriented (wants something) • Stops when they get what they want (or realize they won't) • Aware of audience • Can be redirected MELTDOWN: • Neurological overload • NOT goal-oriented • Cannot stop on command • Not aware of or responsive to audience • Needs safety, space, and time A tantrum is a negotiation. A meltdown is a nervous system crash. You can discipline a tantrum. You can only support a meltdown. Understanding this changed everything for our family. Share this with someone who needs to hear it. #AutismMeltdown #Meltdown #AutismParent #AutismAwareness #SensoryOverload #AutismMom #AutismDad #Neurodiversity

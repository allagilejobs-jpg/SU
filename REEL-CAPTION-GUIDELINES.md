# Reel Caption Guidelines — Spectrum Unlocked

**Last Updated:** April 7, 2026

Reel captions are NOT the same as feed/carousel captions. Different rules, different psychology.

---

## 📏 Length Rules

| Format | Length | Why |
|--------|--------|-----|
| **Reel caption** | 80-150 characters | Above the "more" cutoff. Most people only see the first 125 chars while scrolling. |
| **Feed/carousel caption** | 1,500-2,200 characters | Long-form storytelling. Optimized for saves. |

---

## 🎯 The 6 Reel Caption Rules

### 1. Hook in the first 3-5 words
Instagram cuts off captions at ~125 characters with a "more" link. Those first words are what people see while the reel plays.

✅ *"ABA: both sides parents need to hear."*
❌ *"Today I want to share something I've been thinking about for a while..."*

### 2. Don't repeat what's in the video
The video already says it. The caption should ADD context, ask a question, or drive an action.

### 3. One CTA, not five
Pick ONE: comment, save, share, or follow. Asking for everything = getting nothing.

### 4. Hashtags: 3-5 in caption (NOT 30)
Reel algorithm uses captions, audio, and watch time — NOT hashtag stuffing. Stuffing 30 tags actually hurts reach.

### 5. Captions are read AFTER the video plays
Most people watch the reel first, then glance at the caption. So the caption is a *continuation* of the conversation, not the start.

### 6. No links in captions
Instagram doesn't make them clickable. Use "Link in bio" or "DM me [keyword]".

---

## 📝 Reel Caption Formula

```
[Hook line — 5-10 words, punchy]
[1-line context or CTA — optional]
[3-5 hashtags]
```

**Examples:**

```
ABA therapy. Both sides. No spin.
Save this for your next provider meeting.
#autism #ABA #autismparent #neurodiversity #fyp
```

```
This BBC scene won't leave me. 💔
Watch the full doc — link in bio.
#autismafrica #bbcafricaeye #autismawareness
```

```
5 visual supports your IEP team won't tell you about.
Save and share with another autism parent.
#visualsupports #autismparenting #IEP #autism
```

---

## 🚫 What NOT to Do

❌ Copy/paste your feed caption to a reel
❌ Use 20-30 hashtags
❌ Write 5 paragraphs
❌ Start with "Today I..."
❌ Bury the hook
❌ Ask for save AND share AND follow AND comment in one caption
❌ Include external links (they don't work)

---

## ✅ What WORKS

✅ Hook in first 5 words
✅ Under 150 characters
✅ One specific CTA
✅ 3-5 targeted hashtags
✅ Question or statement, not explanation
✅ Emotion before information

## 🔄 The Two-Caption System

For every reel post, your `captions.md` should have BOTH:

1. **Feed caption** (long, for cross-posting to feed if needed)
2. **Reel caption** (short, for the actual reel post)

This way you can grab whichever you need depending on where you're posting.

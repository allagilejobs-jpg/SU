## 📈 Trending Content Topics (2024-2026)

### High-Performing Topics
1. **Newly diagnosed guidance** — "What to do THIS WEEK after diagnosis"
2. **Sensory-friendly practical tips** — Feeding, clothing, environment
3. **Myth-busting** — Debunking autism stereotypes
4. **Language & vocabulary** — How to talk about autism respectfully
5. **Evidence-based information** — From professionals (doctors, therapists)
6. **Day-in-the-life family content** — Relatable reality
7. **IEP/School advocacy** — Navigating education system
8. **Therapy options** — ABA controversy, alternatives, what works

### Content Gaps (Opportunities)
- **Parent-focused TikTok content** — UNC study shows only ~15% of autism TikTok content is parent-focused, though ~30% of autistic individuals have high support needs. Room for more!
- **Practical templates** — Visual schedules, communication boards, routine charts
- **"What I wish I knew"** — First-person parent retrospectives

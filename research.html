🚀 Content Gaps (Opportunities)

  • Parent-focused TikTok content — UNC study shows only ~15% of autism TikTok content is parent-focused, though ~30% of autistic individuals have high support needs. Room for more!
  • Practical templates — Visual schedules, communication boards, routine charts
  • "What I wish I knew" — First-person parent retrospectives

🔍 Platform Insights

📱 TikTok

  • Autism hashtags heavily used but underrepresent parent/caregiver perspective
  • Evidence-based content from professionals performs well
  • Controversial topics (ABA, diagnosis criteria) drive engagement

📸 Instagram

  • Carousels perform best for educational content
  • Reels for quick tips and relatable moments
  • Stories for polls, Q&A, community building

🎬 YouTube

  • 60+ dedicated autism channels (per Feedspot 2026)
  • Long-form content for deep dives
  • Shorts competing with TikTok/Reels
